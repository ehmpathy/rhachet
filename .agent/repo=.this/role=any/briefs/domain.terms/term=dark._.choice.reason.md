# domain.term.choice.reason: dark

## .etymology

from *a dark lantern* — one that is lit but shows no light. the lane **ran**; the light never
reached you. that is the precise sense: not absent, not broken, but present and unreadable.

chosen over:

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| `silent` | implies the lane said naught. the contentless shape is loud — it emits a tally, a verdict, and a blocker count. it is unreadable, not quiet |
| `empty` | describes a `0/0` clean review, which is the OPPOSITE state and a perfectly good one |
| `missing` | the lane is not absent — it exists, it ran, it holds a budget slot. only its finding is unrecoverable |
| `skipped` | `🌙 skipped` is already a declared verdict for an optional rubric that matched no rules (`contract.reviewer-output`). to reuse it would overload a word onto two concepts |

## .evidence

**shape 1 — overflow, ~30 rounds.** on the keyrack `--reach` drive, lanes r008/r009 returned
`constraint` on context overflow and each round filed the overflow as an accepted fact. one scoped
re-run produced real verdicts in ~2 minutes — `0/0` on one rubric, **3 items** on the other. the
lanes were dark for ~30 rounds and the record showed only that someone had noticed.

**shape 2 — contentless, 2026-09-05.** on the keyrack `@all` drive, lane
`enroll-verif-snapshot-blemishes` at i006 emitted, in full:

> *"0 blockers written to stdout above was informal — restating per contract: 1 blocker, 0 nitpicks."*

a sibling lane confirmed it independently: *"has no recoverable finding text (empty/malformed
review artifact) … it cannot be matched to anything in the current tree."*

the driver read that as a reviewer who disagreed, and spent rounds on `.taken` files addressed to
a point that was never stated. **the takens converged naught, because a contentless block carries
no claim to answer.** the round burned.

⇒ and the lane had been **right**. its rubric was *"experiential and visual blemishes in
snapshotted acceptance test journeys"*; the tree carried a real one — `✋ blocked:` where
`✋ ConstraintError:` belonged, across 80 snapshot occurrences. a human found it by eye hours
later. the lane saw the blemish, lost its text, and the driver read the empty artifact as noise
rather than as a cue to go look.

## .the lesson the word exists to carry

> a dark lane is indistinguishable from a reviewed one **on the ladder**, and indistinguishable
> from a wrong one **in the moment**. so the tell is never the verdict — it is whether a finding
> you could act on came back. when none did, go look yourself.
