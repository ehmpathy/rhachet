# incident: a print enroll with the driver role live drove the route

route · `.behavior/v2026_11_02.fix-boot-briefs-cacheable` · stone 5.3.verification
caught 2026-11-04 · by the driver (self) · harm · none shipped, five minutes of a stray clone

## .what happened

to capture one print turn for the ergonomics self review, the driver ran

```
npx rhx enroll claude -p 'reply with exactly OK' --output-format json --model haiku
```

with no `--roles`. that enrolls the repo actor, driver role live, in the driver's own worktree. the
turn did not exit: its stop hook fed it the bound route's stone, and it drove 5.3 for about five
minutes until the driver stopped the task. it read the yield, ran `route.stone.set`, and tried to write
the very self review the driver was mid-write on.

## .why no damage landed

every write the stray clone tried was refused, and `git status` showed no file it touched; the six
self reviews on disk are the driver's. luck of the gate order, not a guard: had a write been permitted
it would have landed over the driver's work with no lock.

## .the cause

- the rule `rule.forbid.peer-clones-that-carry-the-driver-role` already forbids this, but its cue
  table named only a peer asked a question or a second opinion. a one-shot `-p` turn read as "not a
  peer", so the cue never fired
- the same hazard sat in a paved tool: `rhx boot.cache.probe` defaulted `ROLES` to empty, so a probe
  run from a bound route spawned a print-mode peer with the driver live (r002 blocker.4, i002)

## .what was fixed

- the rule gained a print-turn when-row and this measured case
- `boot.cache.probe` defaults to `--roles -driver`; its help, header and snaps say so. graded `urgent`:
  a probe that drives the caller's route edits the caller's files, a nameable behavioral harm. this is
  the one urgent concession of 5.3, and the reason the stone bought review budget
- the other probes (`boot.compact`, `boot.resume`, `boot.subagent`, `boot.adherence`) already defaulted
  to `-driver`

## .what the foreman may want

- a mechanical guard in place of a cue: `rhx enroll` from inside a bound route, with the driver role
  live and no `--as` of a worktree of its own, could refuse or warn. not built here: it is an enroll
  contract change outside this wish's scope
