# 🌙 dream: the unbudgeted `<stats>` line discloses a 3.29x error with one character

- **kind** = fix
- **owner** = `ehmpathy/rhachet`
- **caught** = 2026-09-25

---

## .the cue that fired

a human read the two `<stats>` shapes side by side in one session's boot and asked why the old one
survives:

```
tokens ≈ 57408 ($0.17 at $3/mil)                  ← every unbudgeted spec
tokens = 4480 (full emitted payload, o200k_base)  ← a spec that declares budget.tokens
```

⇒ the **number** is a known defect with a settled reason (requirement 4 — see below). the
**disclosure** is not. `≈` is the entire signal that the first line is off by **3.29x**, and a
reader takes `≈` to mean a rounded digit.

## .the gap, precisely

`src/domain.operations/boot/asBootStatsLinesUnbudgeted.ts:46-54` renders:

```ts
`  ├── chars = ${input.chars}`,
`  └── tokens ≈ ${tokens} (${asBootCostHuman({ tokens })} at $${COST_PER_MILLION_TOKENS}/mil)`,
```

the file's own docblock states the error plainly — *"measured at 3.29x understated on this repo's
own boot"*, decomposed as a 2.72x scope error × a 1.21x divisor error. **that honesty lives in the
source and reaches no reader of the output.**

| the reader | what they are told |
|---|---|
| a reader of `asBootStatsLinesUnbudgeted.ts` | the exact factor, and both of its causes |
| 🔴 a reader of the rendered line | `≈` |

⇒ and the asymmetry bites hardest on a human who sets a cap. they read `≈ 57408`, pick
`budget: 60_000`, declare it — and the very next boot measures the real payload at ~1.7x that and
halts. the cap was denominated in a unit the render never said was a proxy.

## .why the NUMBER stays — settled, and not this dream's subject

`0.wish.md` requirement 4 pins an unbudgeted render **byte-identical** to today. `F12` records that
the budget-conditional shape was **forced** by it rather than chosen, and
`roles.boot.budget` `[case1]` + the `roles.boot.bootyaml` snapshot set enforce it mechanically.

two costs back it beyond the snapshot churn:

1. a correction changes a line **every** role boot in **every** consumer repo prints
2. an unbudgeted boot constructs no encoder at all (lazy on a declared `budget`). a corrected
   number puts ~1,800ms of encoder construction plus encode on every session's `hook.onBoot`

## 🔴 .why the LABEL cannot be fixed forward here either

the cheap move — leave the arithmetic alone, name the scope in the line — **is also forbidden by
requirement 4**, for the identical reason. requirement 4 pins the *bytes*, not the *number*. a
marker is a byte change, so `[case5][t3]` (which re-runs every extant snapshot) goes red on it
exactly as a corrected digit would.

⇒ the SAFE/CLEAN test (`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`):

| half | SAFE? | CLEAN? | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| the **budgeted** render names its scope + vocabulary | ✅ | ✅ | 🔴 **done this branch** |
| the **unbudgeted** render names its scope | 🟡 no arithmetic changes | 🔴 **no** — requirement 4 forbids the byte change, and a test enforces it | dream it |

## .the shape of the fix

three candidates, cheapest first. **each needs a wisher call, because each relaxes requirement 4:**

1. **a marker in the line** — `tokens ≈ 57408 (say content only, chars/4 estimate)`. one line, and
   it breaks every extant boot snapshot in this repo and downstream
2. **route the correction through the extant lazy load** — an unbudgeted boot already does zero
   tokenizer work; make the accurate number opt-in by a flag rather than by a `budget`, so a human
   who wants the truth need not declare a cap first
3. **do neither, and point at `roles cost`** — which already runs the real counter unconditionally
   (`invokeRolesCost.ts:198-199`) for one role or, with `--all`, for every spec in the repo. the
   honest number is one command away today, and it is undocumented as the remedy

🟡 **option 3 is what the branch actually shipped and never said out loud.** the cheapest real
improvement may be a doc line rather than a render change.

## .why it is not done in this round

requirement 4 is the wish's own text, and `[case5][t3]` is the clamp that holds it. to relax it is a
**scope call the wisher reserved** (`0.wish.md`: *"the requirement table are authoritative"*), not a
mechanism call the wish delegated. ⇒ a fix-forward here would settle by side effect a requirement
the wisher wrote.
