# 🌙 dream: unit and integration tests mint raw os temp dirs and never reclaim them

- kind = dirt
- owner = rhachet
- caught 2026-11-16

## .the cue that fired

the acceptance suite's fixture dirs now mint through `genTempDir`, which the run's autoprune
teardown reclaims (`v2026_11_14.fix-acceptance-suite-shares-the-box`). a grep for `tmpdir()` in
`src/**/*.test.ts` found ~50 sites in the unit and integration tiers that still mint straight in
the os temp root with `mkdtempSync` or `join(tmpdir(), ...)`, and none removes its dir.

## .the gap, precisely

- `src/domain.operations/manifest/getRoleRegistryManifest{,.integration}.test.ts` mint `rhachet-test-*`
- `src/infra/host/getHomeHash.integration.test.ts`, `src/infra/git/getGitRepoRootOrNull.integration.test.ts`,
  `src/infra/ssh/sshPrikeyToAgeIdentity.integration.test.ts`, the keyrack daemon socket test,
  the brains discovery tests, `src/_topublish/**` — each mints its own prefix
- `code.test.accept.blackbox` asks for `genTempDir` in acceptance; no rule asks it of the other
  tiers, and the unit ones that touch the filesystem also cross a remote boundary
  (`rule.forbid.unit.remote-boundaries`)

## .the shape of the fix

per site: `genTempDir({ slug })` (with `realpathSync` where a path is compared to a cli's cwd),
and wire the autoprune setup + teardown in `jest.unit.config.ts` and `jest.integration.config.ts`
as the acceptance config does. `getGitRepoRootOrNull` needs a non-repo dir, so it keeps a dir
outside the repo and removes it in its own `afterAll`. a unit test that writes files moves to the
integration tier.

## .why it is not done in this round

the wish bounds this behavior to the acceptance suite; ~50 sites across two other tiers is a
change of its own, with its own review.
