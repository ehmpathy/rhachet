# 🌙 dream: the renew tick holds its durations as IsoDuration, not `*Ms` numbers

- kind = fix
- owner = rhachet keyrack daemon
- caught 2026-11-25

## .the cue that fired

`rule.forbid.unit-suffixed-time-fields`, written in answer to r4696241582 on ehmpathy/rhachet#759,
fires on the context inputs the renew tick threads through its operations.

## .the gap, precisely

- `scheduleDaemonRenewTick` reads `tickMs`, `windowMs`, `renewTimeoutMs` as numbers
- `renewDaemonGrantsDue` takes `windowMs` and `renewTimeoutMs` in its context
- `getOneRenewalSettled` takes `timeoutMs` in its context
- `getOneDurationMsFromEnv` returns a number by name

## .the shape of the fix

1. `getOneDurationFromEnv({ envVar, fallback: IsoDuration }) → IsoDuration`
2. the context inputs become `window: IsoDuration`, `renewTimeout: IsoDuration`, `timeout: IsoDuration`
3. `toMilliseconds` only at `setInterval` / `setTimeout`, the two raw-ms apis
4. the window comparison via `addDuration` on the `now` stamp

## .why it is not done in this round

it reshapes the context of five operations and their tests, under a review whose ask was the
wire field. the wire field (`expiresIn`) landed; the internal inputs earn their own change.
