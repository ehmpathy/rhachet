## .the cue that fired

a reviewer flagged one unfollowable `.dream/` citation. the audit that followed found **six** this route
wrote, and every one settled the same way: **the work landed, and the citation stayed.** six of one shape
is a pattern, not six incidents.

## .the gap, precisely

`rule.always.catch-dreams-for-followups` (bhrain/role=driver) names one act and one moment:

> at the moment you defer, write a dream and symlink it at the route.

it names **no act at the moment a caught item is instead FIXED.** and that moment is common — the
`philosophy.entoolment`/SAFE-CLEAN pair actively pushes toward it: a deferral is priced from a guess at
the ripple cost, and a guess that proves cheap converts a defer into a fix. `case=F26` is the worked
instance: the clamp looked impossible under `stdio: 'inherit'`, a report-file capture dissolved that, and
the one-line fix landed.

⇒ so **the citation outlives its deferral by default**, and the artifact that carries it then reads as a
false record — *"a deferral was recorded and the record does not exist"* — which grades as an unproven
claim, the class a verification gate forbids at zero tolerance.

🟡 **the asymmetry is the whole defect.** the catch step is loud, mandated, and symlinked. the retire step
is unnamed, so it is skipped by every author who does the right thing and fixes the item.

## .the shape of the fix

1. **the checker — DONE, in this repo.** `.agent/.notes/tool.check-dream-citations.js` walks every `.md`,
   matches each `.dream/…dream.md` citation against disk, reports a broken symlink beside it, and exits 2
   on a defect this tree owns. it is the mechanizable half, and a checker outranks a rule
   (`philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle`) because it needs no author to recall it.
   - 🟡 **it SORTS rather than lumps**, which is what makes it trustworthy: `LIVE` / `RECORD` / `MENTION`
     for a citation, `OURS` / `FOREIGN` / `FIXTURE` / `SCRATCH` for a symlink. four of those seven are
     correct by construction, so a tool that reported them beside a real defect would be hand-filtered
     once and ignored thereafter (`rule.require.clamp-the-premise-a-guard-rests-on`)
2. **wire it** — a `--what lint` row, or a route guard `judges:` entry, so a phantom blocks rather than
   waits for a reviewer to notice one by eye. 🟡 it cannot be wired while the **14 LIVE** items below
   stand, since it would red the gate on a prior debt this wish did not create.
3. **the rule amendment — the reseed.** `rule.always.catch-dreams-for-followups` gains the paired act:
   *when you FIX a caught item instead, retire its dream and every citation of it, or amend the citation
   to state the fix.* the cue table gains a row: *"a caught item turns out cheap enough to fix"* →
   *"retire the dream, sweep its citations."*
4. **settle the 14 LIVE** — one pass per prior route, each item checked against code, each verdict
   recorded. once they are settled, part 2 unblocks and the defect stops to be a matter of attention.

## .why it is not done in this round

the **SAFE/CLEAN** test (`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`) splits the four parts:

| part | SAFE | CLEAN | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — the checker | ✅ additive, reads only | ✅ one new file | 🟢 **done in this round** |
| 2 — wire it | ✅ | 🔴 it reds today on the **14 LIVE** of two prior routes, so a wire now blocks this gate on a debt this wish did not create. it also owes its own clamp — a gate change, at a gate | deferred, on part 4 |
| 3 — the rule | ✅ | 🔴 **another repo.** a tree adopts only what is scoped to itself | reseed |
| 4 — settle the 14 LIVE | 🔴 two CLOSED routes, whose yields are historical records | 🔴 14 per-item code checks across a context this wish never opens | deferred |

⇒ and part 1 is the part that matters most: **the defect is now computable rather than a matter of
attention**, so the next drive finds all 27 with one command instead of by eye.

## .no fulcrum is raised, and why

`rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess` owes a fulcrum where a **dirt deferral** hides a fork
the wisher would want to rule. there is no fork here: parts 2–4 are one shape with one repair, and the
bound that defers them is `rule.forbid.scope-leaks`, which the wisher does not need to re-decide. the
deferral's cost is stated in the table above rather than hidden.
