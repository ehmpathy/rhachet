# 🌙 dream: an enroll that would drive its enroller's bound route is caught at the enroll

kind · fix
owner · rhachet, `src/contract/cli/invokeEnroll.ts`
caught 2026-11-04

## .the cue that fired

a print enroll with no `--roles` drove the bound route for five minutes
(`.foreman/v2026_11_04.incident.a-print-enroll-with-driver-live-drove-the-route.md`). the rule that
forbids it is a cue a reader must recall; the enroll itself said naught.

## .the gap, precisely

`rhx enroll` spawns a clone with the driver role live into a worktree whose route is bound, and the
clone's hooks then hand it that route. no check sits at the enroll.

## .the shape of the fix

at enroll: the worktree has a route bound, the effective roles include `driver`, and the enroll is a
peer (`-p`, `--async`, or `--as @:…`) → refuse with exit 2, a ConstraintError whose hint names
`--roles -driver`, plus an explicit flag for the rare enroll meant to drive. clamp: an acceptance case
red without the check, green with it.

## .why it is not done in this round

an enroll contract change, outside the cache wish; it needs the wisher's call on the override flag.
