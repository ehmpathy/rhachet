# 🌙 dream: a clone gate's `ss` scan runs on after its peer hangs up

kind = reseed · owner = rhachet (clone) · caught 2026-11-01

## .the cue that fired

`v2026_11_01.fix-clone-auth-lookup-timeout` raised the peer-cred lookup bound from 5s to 60s. the
say cli's observe loop opens a probe connection every ~1.25s (1s reply + 250ms poll), and each
connection's gate starts its own `ss -xp`.

## .the gap, precisely

a probe that gives up client-side leaves its scan alive server-side until `ss` exits (≤ 60s). on a
box where `ss` takes T seconds, ~T / 1.25 scans run at once per observed say. the scans add load to
the box whose load made them slow.

## .the shape of the fix

the gate owns an `AbortController`; the socket's `close` aborts it; `isCallerSameUser` passes the
signal to `getSocketPeerCred`, whose `execFileAsync('ss', ['-xp'], …)` takes `{ signal }`. an
aborted lookup settles as a non-pass the server never replies to (the peer is gone). clamp: a probe
with a 1s reply against a fake 9s `ss`; assert the `ss` child is gone within ~1s of the hangup.

the lookup already runs `ss` with no shell, so a `signal` kill lands on `ss` itself, as the bound's
kill does. a clamp that uses the fake `ss` (itself a shell) must assert on the fake's own pid, or
kill its process group, since its `sleep` child can outlive the kill.

## .why it is not done in this round

the wish scopes to the two bounds; the abort is a contract change across three files with its own
clamp.
