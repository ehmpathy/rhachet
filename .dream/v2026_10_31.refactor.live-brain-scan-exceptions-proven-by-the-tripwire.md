   brains only" claim lives in `reason` prose

   `enroll.shared-brain-auth` spawns the cli by absolute path, so its claim stays prose; name it
2. load the caselists and the [case2]/[case3] fixture bodies from `.json` data files, which no scan
   reads; drop the three exceptions and their ~40 names
3. one op, `asLiveBrainMarkersOfPath({ registry, path, at: 'file' | 'collection' })`, owns scope,
   suffix and lazy; both scans and the realjest test call it

each mock suite under the tripwire, (2) moves three test files' data, and (3) touches both scans
