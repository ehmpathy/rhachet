## .the shape of the fix

1. **generate a uuid at spawn** (`getUuid` from `uuid-fns`, already a dep) and pass
   `--session-id <uuid>` in the brain-cli argv.
2. **stamp the exid into the clone record** at spawn, beside `spawnedAt`.
3. **link by the record** — `genCloneHistoryLink` symlinks `<exid>.jsonl` directly. no candidate
   pool, no claim, no quarantine.
4. **retire the heuristic subsystem**, or demote it to a fallback for a brain with no
   `--session-id`: `getAllEligibleTranscriptCandidates` · `genAtomicSymlinkClaim` ·
   `isTranscriptWithinSpawnWindow` · `getAmbiguousExidsWithinSpawnWindow` · the `exidsForeign` and
   `exidsAmbiguous` reports in `getCloneOutput`.
5. **teach the stub brain the flag** — `src/.test/assets/stubBrainCli.cjs` must write
   `<session-id>.jsonl` too, or every integration fixture diverges from its subject
   (`rule.require.a-stub-refuses-what-its-subject-refuses`).

### 🟡 the two premises this fix still rests on

both are measurable with one `-p` run each, and neither is measured:

- **a collision** — does `--session-id` on an id that already exists RESUME that session, refuse, or
  overwrite? a clone must never resume a stranger's conversation, so the answer decides whether the
  uuid needs a claim of its own.
- **a non-claude brain** — the flag is claude's. the fallback in item 4 is what keeps a brain without
  it working, and `getBrainTranscriptDir` already returns `null` for such a brain, so the seam exists.

### the clamp

two clones spawned in ONE cwd, each with its own assigned exid, each link **exactly** its own
transcript and report no ambiguity. it goes red under the extant discovery path, which quarantines
both and links neither.

## .why it is not done in this round

the SAFE/CLEAN test (`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`) fails on both halves:

- **not safe** — it changes how every clone's transcript is identified, which is the input to `get`,
  to `say`'s `released` verdict, and to `prune`. a wrong move gives every clone an empty history, and
  an empty history reads as a deaf clone.
- **not clean** — it ripples into the spawn argv, the clone record shape, the stub brain, and the
  test corpus of six operations whose every fixture is built on the discovery contract. plus the two
  unmeasured premises above.

⇒ and it is **out of the wish's scope**: `0.wish.md` is `clone say`'s verdict set. this is the
identity of a clone's transcript, which that wish consumes and does not define.
