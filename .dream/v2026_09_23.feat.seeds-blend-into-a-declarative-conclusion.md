## .the rule today

`rule.always.archive-the-wishers-words-verbatim` has each wisher utterance land as its own
`.seeds/inventory.of=seeds.case=S$n-*.md` file, with an index of rows beside it.

## .why it fails

- the files accrete as a **chronicle**: 27 on this route, each with `.settled` · `.landed` · `.why it changed the work` narration.
- a later seed overrides an earlier one, and the earlier file still reads as settled. S6 said `--append-system-prompt`; S12 reversed it; S25 refined it. three files, one live answer, and the reader must trace the chain.
- the chronicle is the exact form S19 · S22 strike from every other yield.

## .the rule it should become

- `.seeds/inventory.of=seeds._.md` is **one declarative document**, grouped by topic.
- each topic states what holds, names the seeds it rests on, and resolves every override once — `S6 → S12 → S25`.
- the wisher's words sit beneath each topic as its ground. cleaned of profanity, otherwise faithful.
- a new utterance **edits the conclusion** it changes. it never appends a new file that contradicts an old one.
- no per-case files.
