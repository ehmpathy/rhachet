## .the gap, precisely

## .the shape of the fix

- skill: `rhx route.guard.await --stone <s> [--route <r>] [--timeout <dur>]`
  - finds the inflight round for the stone: the latest arrival with no judged verdict yet
  - blocks until every lane is terminal, with a progress tree per lane (inflight / approved / rejected / malfunction)
  - exits with the judge's verdict and the next move (absorb the named concerns, or `--as passed`)
  - no inflight round → says so, exit 0, and names the latest verdict
  - idempotent: safe to call again, and from another session
- `rhx route.drive` (and `--when hook.onStop`): when the bound stone's guard has an inflight round, replace "choose one" with
  - `🍵 the guard reviews your stone — await it: rhx route.guard.await --stone <s>`
  - keep `--as blocked` as the escape hatch; drop the re-arrive suggestion while inflight
