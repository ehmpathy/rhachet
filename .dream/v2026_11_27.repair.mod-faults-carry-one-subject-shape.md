# 🌙 planned pr: `refactor(mods): a mod fault carries one subject shape`

- kind = repair, as a dedicated pr
- owner = ehmpathy/rhachet
- caught 2026-11-27, in `.behavior/v2026_11_27.fix-mods-never-block-enrollment` (i006 r011, enroll-impl-arch-defects, nitpicks 1, 2, 4); planned as its own pr on 5.3 r1 blocker.1 (fulcrum F17)

## .the cue that fired

three review points on one seam: the shape a mod fault travels in.

## .the gap, precisely

- two errno classifiers: `asModsPathFault` in `getAllRoleModEntries` and `asRoleModFaultOfErrno`
  - their errno sets differ on `EISDIR`, and their metadata shapes differ
  - that split is why `asRoleModFaultLabel` sniffs for `modsDir`
- the fault's `role` key holds a role slug, a stray entry name, or the literal `.mods`
- `syncRoleModdedViews` returns `refusals`, `skipped` and `unplaced`, with `skipped ∪ unplaced = refusals` held by a closure alone; `Array<{ role; error }>` is spelled about ten times

no defect rides with it: the stray-vs-role collision is fixed in the route that found it (`getAllRoleModFaultsOfRepo` filters on unplaced slugs, clamped by its case3).

## .the shape of the fix — the bounded diff

1. one `RoleModFault = { subject, error }` domain literal, the label minted once where the fault arises
2. `getAllRoleModEntries` calls `asRoleModFaultOfErrno` with a subject; `asModsPathFault` is deleted; `asRoleModFaultLabel` stops its sniff
3. the sync returns `skipped` and `unplaced` only; check first that no reader outside the orphan dedupe reads `refusals`
4. every reader moves onto `RoleModFault`: the 32 files under `src/` that read the fault shape today, and their snapshots
5. the clamp: each readout snapshot holds byte for byte, since the reshape changes no human surface

## .why it is its own pr

it reshapes the fault type every mods reader reads — the render, the sweep, five readouts, the sync, and two cli commands. that is a core-contract change across many callers, which earns a focused review of its own (`rule.forbid.deferrals-short-of-a-dedicated-pr`, the one exception).
