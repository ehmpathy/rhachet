## .the cue that fired

the i004 round of `beav/feat-actors-yml` (enroll-impl-arch-defects) named six moves that change no
behavior, and the route held them out of that change (fulcrum F21).

## .the gap, precisely

1. `setDeltaActorsOndisk`, `setDeclaredActorsOndisk` and `syncActiveActorBrainDirs` each enumerate
   actors and own their own predicate (`asActorsDelta`, `asActorsDeclared`,
   `asActorsExceptRepoActor`); `asFailuresWithEnumerationOnce` exists only to dedupe their failures
2. the refusal "`@x` declares role '…', which is not linked" lives twice —
   `asRoleRefsOfActorExpanded` and `asRoleSlugLinkedOrThrow` (in `asActorFormEnrollment`) — and the
   two list roles in different orders (`refsLinked` vs `expanded.roles`)
3. `enroll/genBrainCliConfigArtifact` imports `init/hooks/…`, while `init` imports
   `enroll/setActorSettingsDerived` (F16 holds the first edge)
4. `actor.json` is joined by hand at 7 production sites
5. `asActorDeclared` (`actor/manifest`) imports `isSafeCloneSlug` from `clone/`
6. `invokeInit` holds two refusal rules, the plan, and dynamic imports

## .the shape of the fix

1. one `asActorsPartitioned({ actors, manifest })` → `{ repo, declared, delta, hash }`, read once in
   `syncBootsForBrainDirs`; delete the dedupe
2. one `getOneRoleRefLinkedOfSpecifier`, one order (`expanded.roles`), one test
3. move the actor-settings derivation under `actor/enrolled/`
4. one `getActorOndiskManifestPath({ repoPath, exid })`
5. move the slug charset check to a neutral `actor/` or `utils/` home
6. one `asInitRefusalOfManifest` transformer, called once from `invokeInit`

## .why it is not done in this round

each move opens files the change did not otherwise touch, and none changes what a human sees — a
large cleanup inside an unrelated change, which scouts honor refuses. move 2 has a real seam (the
order), so it is the first to pull in.
