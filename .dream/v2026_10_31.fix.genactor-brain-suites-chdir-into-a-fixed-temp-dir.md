# 🌙 dream: the genActor.brain suites chdir into a fixed temp dir

kind = fix · owner = rhachet · caught 2026-10-31

## .the cue that fired

peer review of `v2026_10_30.fix-no-test-bills-the-anthropic-api` (5.1, i003 r007) flagged `process.chdir(tmpDir)` and a fixed `__dirname`-relative `./.temp/case*` dir in `genActor.brain.{casePublished,caseCollocated,caseAskable}.integration.test.ts`.

## .the gap, precisely

each suite `rmSync`s and recreates a fixed dir under `src/contract/sdk/.temp/`, then `process.chdir`s into it for the whole given. two runs on one checkout race on the dir, and any test that shares the worker reads the changed cwd. both shapes are on `main`; that branch only wrapped the suites in the tier-3 gate and bounded their repl.

## .the shape of the fix

- a per-run dir: `genTempDir({ slug })` under the repo (the comment says the dir stays in the project to dodge a brain-cli trust prompt; `genTempDir` keeps that if it lands under the repo)
- pass the cwd to `genActor` / the brain rather than `process.chdir`, if the brain contract accepts one; else spawn the act in a child with its own cwd

## .why it is not done in this round

the cwd is how the brain finds the example repo's roles; to drop `process.chdir` means a look at whether `genActor` and the repl take a cwd, which is a contract question beyond a cost-gate change. the suites are now tier-3 (flag-run), so the race needs two flagged runs at once on one checkout.
