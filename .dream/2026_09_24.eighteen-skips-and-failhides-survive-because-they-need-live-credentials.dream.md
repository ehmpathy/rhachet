## .the shape of the fix

two independent repairs, neither coupled to the other:

| # | population | the move |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the six failhides | put `MOCK_OP_CLI_DIR` on `PATH` the way `MOCK_GH_CLI_DIR` already is, so `opAvailable` is true by construction and the six guards delete outright |
| 2 | the twelve brain skips | convert each to a declared env gate — `given.runIf(BRAIN_LIVE)` — the shape the perf suites already use, and wire `BRAIN_LIVE=true` into a credentialed lane |

🔴 **repair 1 is the one to take first, and it is the smaller of the two.** the asset exists, the
precedent is in the same file for `gh`, and it converts six fake passes into six real assertions with
no credential at all.

🟡 **repair 2 is a downgrade in strictness on its face and an upgrade in honesty.** a `.skip` claims
naught; a `runIf` names the condition under which the case is real, which is what makes a
credentialed lane buildable later.

## .why it is not done in this round

the SAFE/CLEAN test (`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`):

| question | answer |
|---|---|
| **SAFE?** | 🔴 **no.** repair 1 changes what six acceptance cases exercise — a mock's behavior may diverge from real `op`, and this behavior touched neither keyrack nor 1Password |
| **CLEAN?** | 🔴 **no.** 18 sites across 9 files, every one outside this behavior's diff, and repair 2 needs a credentialed CI lane that does not exist |

⇒ row 2 of the rule's verdict table — **defer, and raise a fulcrum**, since the dirt call is a
judgment about ripple cost rather than about size. the fulcrum is `F44`.

⚠️ **the deferral is disclosed rather than silent.** `5.3.verification.yield.md` carries the scan's
full result under its zero-skips section, so a reviewer meets the 18 sites in the gate's own artifact
instead of via a grep.
