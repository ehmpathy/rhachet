## .the cue that fired

the judge reported a clean blocker tally and refused anyway:

```
disputed: 0 blockers, 1 nitpicks excluded from the tally
residual: 0/0 blockers, 10/0 nitpicks

passed: false
reason: nitpicks exceed threshold (10 > 0)
```

every one of those concerns was **absorbed**. the absorb gate was satisfied. and six of the ten were
items a fulcrum (`F35`) had **deferred on a stated warrant** — so the fix they were conceded toward
would not arrive in this round, nor the next, nor any round of this stone.

## 🔴 .the gap, precisely

`rule.always.absorb-every-concern` states both verbs plainly:

| disposition | commits you to |
|---|---|
| `conceded` — the default | **fix it, run again** |
| `disputed` — the escalation | **shed from the tally; council rules** (cite a fulcrum) |

and it names the dispute case in four words: **"fine to continue — cite a fulcrum."**

⇒ **a deferral IS that case.** the concern is correct, the repair is real, and the warrant to not
take it now is written in a fulcrum row the council will read. it is the textbook `disputed`.

🔴 **what the rule does not say — and what cost a round here — is that the choice is IRREVERSIBLE.**

```
$ rhx route.stone.set … --as disputed --with enroll-impl-arch-defects --about nitpick.1 --why …F35….md

error: BadRequestError: enroll-impl-arch-defects's nitpick.1 is already conceded

a stance is final for its given. that given is unchanged, so the stance stands:
  ├─ change the artifact and re-arrive, then take a stance on the new given
  └─ or ask a human to void it now
     └─ rhx route.stone.set --stone … --as rewound
```

⇒ so a concession on a deferred item is not merely the wrong verb. it is **an unclearable line on
the tally for the whole life of that given**, and the only driver-owned remedy is a full extra round
— every lane re-run, every budget spent — to reach a fresh given on which the right verb is legal.

## 🔴 .why `conceded` is the one that pulls

three forces, and each of them pulls the same way:

| the pull | why it wins in the moment |
|---|---|
| the rule names `conceded` **the default** and `disputed` **the escalation** | a driver who is not sure reaches for the default, correctly, as a matter of discipline |
| a dispute **reads as disagreement** | the concern is *right*. to dispute it feels like a denial of a true report, where in fact it denies only **when** the repair lands |
| 🟡 a concession is **cheap at the moment it is made** | the whole cost lands later, at a judge gate, with no line that points back at the verb |

⇒ and the bias is correct **for an ordinary concern**. it inverts for exactly one class: a concern
whose repair is **already deferred by a fulcrum**. there, `conceded` is a promise the round has
already decided not to keep.

## .the shape of the fix

**a cue, in `rule.always.absorb-every-concern`'s when-then table:**

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| a concern's repair is **deferred**, with a fulcrum that records the deferral | 🔴 `disputed`, `--why` that fulcrum. **never `conceded`** — a concession promises a fix this round, and the round already decided to make none |
| you would concede a concern whose fix is a dream you just caught | 🔴 the same cue. the dream is the evidence the repair will not land here |
| you are unsure which verb a concern earns | ⚠️ **the choice is FINAL for that given.** a wrong `conceded` costs a full round to correct; a wrong `disputed` is read by the council |

**and a line in `howdoes.the-review-gates-you-drive-within`**, under gate 1, since that document is
the map a driver reads before the absorb loop rather than mid-loop:

> **a stance is final for its given.** the engine refuses a second disposition on the same concern
> of the same given. to correct one, change the artifact and re-arrive — or ask a human to `rewind`.

🟡 **the asymmetry is what makes the cue earn its place.** the two errors do not cost the same:

- a wrong **`disputed`** is visible — it lands in front of the council with its fulcrum attached,
  and the council can simply rule against it
- a wrong **`conceded`** is invisible until the judge refuses, and its only driver-owned remedy is
  to spend a whole round of every lane's budget

⇒ **so the tie-break should favour `disputed` wherever a fulcrum already exists**, which is the
opposite of what the default alone suggests.
