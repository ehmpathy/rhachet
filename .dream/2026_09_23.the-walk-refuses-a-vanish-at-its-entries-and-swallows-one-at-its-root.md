# 🌙 dream: the walk REFUSES a vanish at its entries and SWALLOWS one at its root

- **kind** = defect
- **owner** = `ehmpathy/rhachet` — `src/infra/filesystem/getAllFilesFromDir.ts`
- **caught** = 2026-09-23, at `5.1.execution.from_vision` i016 (`enroll-impl-arch-defects` nitpick)

## .the cue that fired

a lane read the walk end to end and named one arm by line:

> the root-open `realpathSync` `ENOENT` arm returns `[]` silently, while the per-entry loop
> throws on the identical race a few lines below — an asymmetric undercount on the budget
> gate's own denominator.

🔴 **the read is correct, and it is short by one site.** the same swallow sits at the
`readdirSync` open too, so the walk carries the defect at **two** root-level arms and refuses it
at exactly one entry-level arm.

## .the gap, precisely

`F28` settled that a vanish is REFUSED rather than skipped, and the repair landed on the entry
loop alone. the three `ENOENT` arms now disagree:

| # | arm | a legitimate absence? | a vanish race? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `existsSync(dir)` → `[]` | ✅ **yes, and this arm is correct** — a role with no `skills/` dir is normal | n/a, it is the first touch |
| 2 | `realpathSync(dir)` → `[]` | 🔴 **no** — arm 1 already answered that | 🔴 **swallowed** |
| 3 | `readdirSync(dir)` → `[]` | 🔴 **no** — arms 1 and 2 already answered that | 🔴 **swallowed** |
| 4 | `statSync(entry)` | a broken symlink → skip, parted by `lstatSync` | ✅ **REFUSED** — `ConstraintError` |

⇒ **arms 2 and 3 are downstream of the legitimate-absence check**, so an `ENOENT` there can only
mean the dir passed a check and then went away. that is the check-then-read race, and the `[]`
is an **undercount** of the very payload the budget gate measures — the one direction
requirement 7 exists to eliminate.

### 🔴 the docblock asserted the opposite, and the assertion carried weight

arm 3 carried, verbatim: *"the skip matches the per-entry `statSync` guard below."* **it did,
until `F28`'s repair changed the entry guard and left this note alone.** a reader who came to
check exactly this asymmetry was told, at the site, that there was none.

⚠️ **that is the sharper half of what the lane found.** a swallowed race is a narrow hole; a
docblock that certifies the hole shut is what kept eleven rounds of re-read off it.

⇒ both notes are now corrected in place. **the prose is repaired; the behavior is not.**

## .the shape of the fix

arms 2 and 3 throw the same `ConstraintError` the entry arm throws:

```ts
throw new ConstraintError('a directory vanished mid-walk', {
  path: dir,
  why: 'the dir passed its own existence check, then was removed or relinked',
  fix: 're-run — a settled tree walks cleanly',
});
```

🟡 **no `lstatSync` split is owed here, and that is the one way it is EASIER than the entry
arm.** arm 1 already absorbed every legitimate absence, so arms 2 and 3 have one cause each.

## 🔴 .why it is not done in this round — the CLAMP cannot be written, not the fix

**SAFE 🟡 / CLEAN 🔴**, and the un-clean half is unusual: it is the test rather than the code.

`rule.require.clamp-edge-cases` demands a regression test that goes **red** under the un-fixed
defect. **this window is not statically constructible:**

| the candidate fixture | why it does not reach arm 2 or 3 |
|---|---|
| a broken symlink | `existsSync` follows links, so arm 1 returns `[]` first |
| a symlink loop | `realpathSync` raises **`ELOOP`**, never `ENOENT` |
| a `chmod 000` dir | raises **`EACCES`**, never `ENOENT` |
| a dir removed before the call | arm 1 catches it — the legitimate-absence path |

⇒ **only a true race reaches these arms**, and a race is not a fixture. a red-able clamp needs
an **injected fs seam** this operation does not carry — which is a contract change to an
`src/infra/` arm that **five** callers share.

🔴 **and the five-caller argument cuts the other way from `F28`'s.** there the repair was a
one-line throw with a `lstatSync` fixture behind it; here the repair is one line and the
**clamp** is a dependency-injection refactor of shared infra. to land the throw with no red-able
clamp is to ship exactly the change `rule.require.clamp-edge-cases` names — protection that
reads as protection and guards naught.

⇒ **so the deferral is on the clamp's cost, never on the fix's correctness.** the fix is right
and it is six lines; what it cannot have today is proof.

## 🔴 .a NEIGHBOUR fault at arm 3 was found constructible, and is CLOSED — 2026-09-23

the deferral above stands **for `ENOENT` alone.** while it sat, a second fault was found at the same
`readdirSync` call — **`ENOTDIR`, raised when `dir` IS A FILE** — and that one needs no seam:

| | the `ENOENT` vanish | 🔴 the `ENOTDIR` file |
|---|---|---|
| reached by | a true race, in a microsecond window | 🔴 **a `writeFileSync` and a call** |
| statically constructible? | 🔴 no — hence the deferral | ✅ **yes** |
| what it shipped | a silent undercount | a bare `Error` ⇒ **exit 1** for a caller-fixable path |
| status | **still deferred**, on the bar above | ✅ **repaired + clamped**, teeth proven by revert |

the repair is a second narrow at the same site, and it keeps the errno set closed
(`rule.forbid.failhide`). `[case4]` of the suite clamps it at three rows: the class is
`ConstraintError`, the message names what it found and how to repair it, and it does **not** return
`[]` — that last row pins the skip as `ENOENT`-only, so a widened narrow reddens it.

### 🔴 .the lesson — a "cannot be clamped" verdict is keyed to the FAULT, never to the ARM

the table under *"why it is not done in this round"* is titled by fixture and reads as a verdict on
**arms 2 and 3**. it is not. every row there probes for `ENOENT`, so what it established is narrower
than what it appeared to say:

> ✅ **what it proved:** no cheap fixture reaches `ENOENT` at arms 2 or 3.
> 🔴 **what it read as:** arms 2 and 3 cannot be clamped without a seam.

⇒ and the gap between those two sentences hid a **live exit-code defect at one of the two arms** for
as long as the deferral held. a reader who arrived at arm 3 to ask *"what else can this raise?"* was
answered by a table that had asked about one errno.

🟡 **the check it earns is one line: before a `needs-a-seam` deferral, enumerate the errnos the call
can raise — never only the one the concern named.** a concern is an entry point to the call site,
not a census of it.

⚠️ **and the same read applies upward.** `F36` defers three shell-skill exits on this identical bar,
and its own flip condition is *"a portable fixture for any one of the three"*. ⇒ this is that
condition met **at a peer site**, which does not flip `F36` and does weaken its premise: *"no
portable fixture exists"* is a claim about a search, and the search here was per-errno rather than
per-call.

## 🔴 .the DI precedent was cited for this seam, and read closely it argues the other way — 2026-09-24

a later lane re-raised the deferral with one new argument, and it is the strongest one offered so
far:

> the precedent (DI seam for a hard-to-trigger boundary) is already in this diff — see
> `getOneBrainTokenCounter`. I'd treat this as buildable now rather than indefinitely deferred.

⇒ the precedent is real, and **what it is a precedent FOR is the opposite of this seam**:

| | `getOneBrainTokenCounter` | the proposed fs seam |
|---|---|---|
| why the seam exists | 🔴 **a PRODUCTION requirement** — `js-tiktoken` must stay out of the bun binary's eval graph, or the `under 250ms` perf invariant breaks | 🔴 **a test.** there is no production driver at all |
| what it owns | a real boundary, named once, with one owner | naught — `node:fs` is already one module |
| the test benefit | 🟡 a **consequence**, stated second in its own docblock | the entire motive |
| the call shape it needed | the callers already took a `context` — the paved `(input, context)` pattern | 🔴 `getAllFilesFromDir(dir, ancestors)` is **positional with no context**, so the seam forces a signature change across five callers |

⇒ so the honest read is: **that seam would have landed with no test in sight**, and this one would
not. `rule.prefer.fewer-paths-via-idempotency` names exactly the second shape — a branch that exists
only for a test to drive — and it is the same objection `F36`'s option A was refused under.

🟡 **the lesson generalizes past this row: a precedent is a claim about WHY a shape was taken, never
about the shape.** two operations can end up with the same signature for opposite reasons, and a
citation that matches on the shape alone imports none of the warrant.

⚠️ **and none of this refutes the DEFECT**, which stands exactly as the table above states it. what
it refutes is that the cost of the clamp has fallen. ⇒ the fix is still right, still six lines, and
still without proof.
