## .the cue that fired

a review nitpick sent me to strip wisher quotes from two term `.reason` files. i held the `.min` of
`rule.forbid.verbatim-quotes-outside-seeds` in booted context, read that it names `.reason` as a
violation site — and then **talked myself out of it**, on the strength of `rule.require.timeless-lessons`,
which says a `.reason` file *"IS an ejection target"*, is *"dated by contract"*, and its dated evidence is
*"mandatory"*.

so i recorded the determination as unresolved and moved on. the full brief settles it in one clause i
could not see:

```md
⇒ see also: … · `rule.require.timeless-lessons` (its `.said` false-positive, which this rule bounds) · …
```

## .the gap, precisely

the condense step drops the entry. checkable, both files on disk at `rhachet-roles-bhrain@0.37.0`:

| artifact | `.see also` entries |
|---|---|
| `rule.forbid.verbatim-quotes-outside-seeds.md` | **5** — the last two are `rule.require.timeless-lessons` and `im_an.obsessive_learner.for.domain.invariants` |
| `…md.min` | **4** — `rule.require.timeless-lessons` is absent |

⚠️ **the min is not wrong about the rule.** it carries the `.reason` violation site twice — once in its
lead (*"a brief, a rule, an invariant, a yield, a fulcrum, a term's `.reason`"*) and once in its boundary
table. so this is not a dropped claim.

⇒ 🔴 **what it drops is the entry that PRE-EMPTS the counter-argument.** the two rules genuinely appear
to conflict, and exactly one artifact says which bounds which — the parenthetical the condense cut.

### 🔴 why a `.see also` entry can be decisive

`rule.prefer.rules-that-refout` grades a rule by cue-vs-argument and sends the argument to `ref` tier. a
`.see also` entry reads as neither — it is navigation, so it sorts as the cheapest line on the page.

but an entry whose parenthetical names a **precedence** relation is not navigation:

| the entry | what it does |
|---|---|
| `rule.require.archaeology-in-notes` | navigation — a peer worth a read |
| `rule.require.timeless-lessons` **(its `.said` false-positive, which this rule bounds)** | 🔴 a **precedence verdict** between two rules that both fire on one artifact |

⇒ the second is the only place in the canon that resolves the conflict. cut it and a reader who holds the
`.min` has two rules, no order, and no signal that the order was ever decided.

### the harm, measured on me

| cost | what it did |
|---|---|
| **a wrong hypothesis, held** | i recorded the `.reason` files as *"likely legitimate"* — the opposite of the verdict |
| **a full-brief read to recover** | the `.min` is booted precisely so a full read is unnecessary; here it was the only way out |
| **a near-miss** | had i not read the full, four files would have shipped the violation past a lane that raised it |

⇒ the shape generalizes: **a reader of a `.min` cannot know a cross-reference was dropped.** an absent
entry and a rule with no such peer render identically.

## .the shape of the fix

the condense step needs one rule about `.see also`: **an entry whose parenthetical states a relation TO
THIS RULE is content, not navigation, and survives.**

| candidate | what it does | cost |
|---|---|---|
| **A — keep every `.see also` entry, always** | the section is never condensed | a long tail of pure-navigation entries on every `.min`; the tier's whole point is a bounded footprint |
| **B — keep an entry whose parenthetical names a relation** | a bare path may drop; `(which this rule bounds)`, `(the clamp)`, `(the positive peer)` stays | needs the parenthetical to be present and honest — an author who writes a bare path loses the protection |
| **C — hoist the precedence verdict out of `.see also`** | a rule that bounds a peer earns a line in the body, where condense already preserves claims | the strongest, and it asks every rule with a bounded peer to be re-edited |

⇒ **B is the cheap correct move and C is the durable one.** they compose: B protects the extant corpus
with no edits; C makes the next rule's verdict reachable whatever the condense does.

🟡 **and the validation is mechanizable** — a `.min` whose `.see also` entry count is below its source's
is detectable without a read, so it is a checker rather than a promise.

## .why it is not done in this round

the SAFE/CLEAN test (`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`):

- **SAFE — n/a here.** the defect is in `rhachet-roles-bhrain`'s condense step and its published `.min`
  artifacts. there is no edit in THIS repo that fixes it — the `.min` i read lives under
  `node_modules/`, regenerated upstream
- ⇒ so it is not a deferral at all: it is **out of this tree's ownership**. a tree adopts only what is
  scoped to itself (`rule.always.scope-onetime-lessons-to-the-behavior`), so the honest artifact is a
  reseed rather than a fix

what this round DID do, in scope: repaired the four artifacts the verdict governs —
`term=buffered._.choice.reason.md` and `term=enqueued._.choice.reason.md` lost the attributed quote,
and the two `._.choice._.md` `.see also` lines that advertised *"the wisher's word"* now name what the
files actually carry.
