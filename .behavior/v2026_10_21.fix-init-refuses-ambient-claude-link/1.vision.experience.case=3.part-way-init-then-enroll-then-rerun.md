# case 3 — a part-way init, then enroll, then a rerun (sharp × criti, densest)

## .narrative

i am the commander. my fresh clone carries the repo actor's `settings.of.repo.json` from git, with
hooks for every role. the install runs; the first role links, and its init fails. today the run
aborts there: one role linked, a file full of other roles' hooks, and enroll refuses on them.

after: init still syncs the hooks to the roles it did link, then fails loud. the error names the role
that failed, the roles it linked, and the roles it did not. i can enroll at once with the crew that
linked — enroll meets no hook of a role it lacks. i fix the failed init and rerun the same command;
every role links, and every role's hooks return.

## .timeline

```
given('[case3] a settings.of.repo.json that holds hooks of roles tester and reviewer, as from git')
  when('[t0] rhx init --hooks --roles tester reviewer, and the tester init exits 1')
    then('exit ≠ 0; the error names repo=…/role=tester and the roles not linked (reviewer)')
    then('settings.of.repo.json holds no hook of reviewer')
    then('settings.of.repo.json holds the hooks of tester (linked before its init ran — fulcrum F1)')
  when('[t1] rhx enroll claude')
    then('no refusal on hooks of roles not enrolled')
  when('[t2] the tester init is fixed, and the same init reruns')
    then('exit 0')
    then('hooks of tester and reviewer both present')
```

## .fail-safe

fast — at the failed role's boundary, as today: no further role links or inits run (F5's best guess).
one step does run after it, by design: the hook sync to the linked roles. that is a deliberate trade —
consistency over the last few seconds of speed — since an abort with no sync is exactly what leaves a
tree enroll refuses (cause 2). the sync touches only `settings.of.repo.json`, under its mutex.

loud (exit ≠ 0, the failed role named), safe (the settings match the linked set, so enroll's guard
holds without a refusal), teaches (the hint is the rerun). clamps inventory case=11; keeps case=9
(a clone loads no hook of a role it is not enrolled in).

## .boundaries crossed

role loop → hook sync → enroll's guard (`assertCloneSettingsLayersHoldOnlyEnrolledRoles`) → a rerun.
