# case 2 — a brain dir init cannot write stops at the door (sharp × criti)

## .narrative

i am the commander, on a box where the repo actor's brain dir came in read-only. i run the install.
before any role links, init probes the dir and its `settings.of.repo.json`, finds it cannot write, and
stops. it names the dir, the probe that failed, and that no role ran. i grant write, rerun, and it
boots. no half-linked roles, no settings file to diff.

## .timeline

```
given('[case2] the repo actor brain dir is read-only')
  when('[t0] rhx init --hooks --roles tester reviewer')
    then('exit 2')
    then('stderr: ✋ ConstraintError: the repo actor brain dir is not writable — names the dir and the probe')
    then('no role is linked; no role init ran')
    then('settings.of.repo.json is byte-equal to before')
  when('[t1] write is granted, and the same init reruns')
    then('exit 0')
```

## .fail-safe

fast (before any role), loud (exit 2, a classed error), teaches (the hint names the dir and the rerun).
clamps inventory case=10.
