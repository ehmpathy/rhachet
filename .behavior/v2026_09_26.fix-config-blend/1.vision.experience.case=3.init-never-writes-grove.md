# case 3 — vlad's global settings survive every `rhx init` (e5, sharp, fail-safe spotlight)

## narrative

vlad keeps his box defaults in `~/.claude/settings.json`: his model, his env, a deny on `Agent`. every actor
in every repo on his box now has a `settings.json` that points there. he links a new role and runs
`rhx init --hooks` in three repos, one after another, as he always has.

afterward he opens `~/.claude/settings.json`. it is exactly as he left it — no role hooks, no repo
permissions, not one byte changed. the new hook shows up only in the `settings.repo.json` of the actors that
hold that role, and `ls -l` shows every actor's `settings.json` still points at the grove.

(the hazard this guards: a hook writer that wrote `<dir>/.claude/settings.json` would follow the arrow into
his global file. the writer now names only `settings.repo.json`, so that write has no path.)
