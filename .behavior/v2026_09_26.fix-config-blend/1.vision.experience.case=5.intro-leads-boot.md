a fresh clone boots. before any role brief, it reads a few lines from rhachet: it is enrolled; `rhx list`
shows every skill its roles grant; `rhx <skill> --help` shows how to call one; the other rhachet commands at a
glance; and when it finds a tactic worth repeat, it makes a skill of it. its first move on a new task is
`rhx list`, not an improvised shell chain. an everyday `claude` (→ `rhx enroll claude`) sees the same intro, from the repo
actor's boot.
