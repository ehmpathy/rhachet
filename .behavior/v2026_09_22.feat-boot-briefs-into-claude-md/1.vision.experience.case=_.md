## .the verdict key

| verdict | what it declares |
|---|---|
| **demoed** | a critipath — owes a `case=N` narrative + bdd sketch |
| **demoed (alter)** | an **alterpath** a dense journey covers anyway. severity stays a nitpick (care); the demo exists because density is high (`define.experience._.metric=boundary-density`) |
| **itemized** | a real alterpath — named here, demo deferred (a nitpick, not a blocker) |
| **forbidden** | a **nurture** bar — the cell *could* occur; a rule we chose forbids it. the row holds the rule, and a later decision could lift it |
| **impossible** | a **nature** bar — the cell *cannot* occur; the mechanism refuses it, and no decision of ours could permit it. the row holds the mechanism |
| 🌙 **deferred** | a **real critipath whose fail-safe is ruled OUT of this behavior's scope.** the cell is itemized and its hazard is walked; **the demo obligation is NOT discharged.** the row names the deferred behavior, the target its fail-safe must hit, and a re-walk obligation keyed to that behavior |

**`deferred` is its own verdict because a hazard walk discharges no coverage.**
`rule.require.experience-coverage` holds that *"a sharp critipath shown unhandled — the edge exists
but no fail-safe is shown — is a blocker."* a deferral may be legitimate (S8 is a wisher scope
call) and still owe the bar. ⇒ **a scope call may move work out of a behavior; it may not move a
coverage bar with it.**

**a barred cell must say WHICH bar it is.** *forbidden* and *impossible* differ in what would
overturn them: a `forbidden` cell is re-openable by a decision; an `impossible` cell is re-openable
only by a change in the mechanism it rests on. ⇒ this inventory holds exactly one barred cell, and
it is `impossible` by nature: **#5**, barred by invariant 1. its unattended twin is barred by the
same mechanism, so the bar is attendance-independent.

**and #5 is the sharpest demonstration of why the bar's KIND must be recorded beside the
verdict.** the mechanism beneath it changed outright at F1's reversal — it was *the prompt is
recorded once per session*; it is now *a hash actor's role change lands at a different dir, so this
session's file is byte-unchanged forever*. **an `impossible` cell is re-openable only by a change in
its mechanism, and its mechanism changed** — so the cell was re-walked rather than assumed, and the
bar survived on a different ground. ⚠️ **it survived only on the HASH form**; on a slug or derived
actor the path holds and the content is rewritten, so there the cell is **open** (`[research]` row
1). `rhx enroll` produces hash actors, so the bar holds for the fleet's default and for no other
form.

**care and density are orthogonal.** care sets *severity*; density sets *where to spend demo
effort*. an alterpath can be the most valuable demo in the set while its severity stays put.

## .feel × care

**feel** — `happy` (behaves as expected) or `sharp` (meets a constraint).
**care** — `criti` (frequent × costly to get wrong) or `alter` (rare, or cheap to recover).

## .the promotion of cell #16 — the argument, settled

| the critipath test (`define.experience._.axis=care.path=criti-vs-alter`) | #16 |
