# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

the experience space, factored into orthogonal axes and walked as a product. the cells are
verdicted in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`; each critipath is demonstrated in a `case=N` file.

## .the axes

### D1 — corpus (2 positions)

**which body of briefs is in play.** this axis is *discovered, not invented*: the wisher named it,
and the mechanism confirms it is a real seam rather than a convenience.

| position | binds at | changes mid-session | transport layer |
|---|---|---|---|
| `role` | clone **enrollment** | ❌ never | layer 2 — `<brainDir>/AGENTS.md`, cached prefix |
| `adhoc` | **route progression** | ✅ on purpose | layer 3 — conversation, append-only |

**this axis carries the weight.** a transport decision made once is wrong; it must be made per
position. the two positions have *opposite* requirements — one wants permanence in the prefix, the
other wants immediate effect outside it.

### D2 — moment (4 positions)

**when in the lifecycle the experience happens.**

| position | what occurs | who acts |
|---|---|---|
| `bind` | the corpus is authored / resolved / rendered to its transport | the enroller — a human at a terminal, or a CI job |
| `deliver` | the corpus reaches the clone's context | the cli |
| `change` | the corpus is meant to differ from what was delivered | whoever drives the route — a human, or a clone — **or a package manager, from outside every session** (see below) |
| `survive` | `/compact`, `/clear`, or `--resume` passes over the session | the cli |

#### 🔴 `change` carries TWO senses, and they SPLIT on the decisive case — found at 3.3.1.blueprint

the `who acts` column above named only in-session performers until 2026-09-23, and that omission
hid a whole sub-axis:

> **WHAT changed — the DECLARATION (which roles the actor is enrolled in), or the SOURCE (the
> content of the briefs those roles name)?**

| the sense | the performer | what it does to the actor's identity |
|---|---|---|
| **declaration** — `rhx enroll --roles +architect` | a human, or a clone, in-session | 🔴 **remints the hash.** `genEnrollmentHash` digests `{ brain, roles }` and `roles` are **slugs**, so a set change lands at a **different** `actor.via.hash=` dir |
| **source** — `pnpm up rhachet-roles-bhrain` | a **package manager**, outside every session | ✅ **the hash is UNCHANGED** — the slugs never moved. same dir, same file, and a re-render **can** land in place |

⇒ 🔴 **the census's `role · change · happy` cell (#5) grades `impossible (nature)`, and its whole
argument is the first row** — *"a hash actor's role change lands at a different dir, so this
session's file is byte-unchanged forever."* **that argument does not reach the second row at all.**

⚠️ and the second row is **not hypothetical**: it is `F8`'s ruled subject, and
`3.3.1.blueprint.product` designs a component (`syncActiveActorBrainDirs`, over `getAllActorsOndisk` + `isActorActive`) and an integration test
for it. ⇒ a test exercises a path the catalog never named — the backward drift
`rule.require.experience-catalog-evolution` grades a blocker.

🟡 **the sub-axis is NOT promoted to a fifth dimension**, for the reason `role count` was rejected
below: it bites at **one** position. `adhoc · change` has no package — a route manifest is authored
in-repo, so its declaration and its source are one artifact. ⇒ the walk adds cells at
`role · change` only, and the census carries them as **#27 · #28 · #29**.

⚠️ **`who acts` names the PERFORMER, never the AUDIENCE.** the performer of an act and the reader
of its output are two different questions: D2 answers the first, D4 answers the second. to fill
this column with *who is present* asserts that a human attends `bind` and does not attend
`deliver`, which contradicts D4 directly — **every moment can occur unattended**, and cell #17 is a
`bind` walked with no human in the room.

### D3 — feel (2 positions)

`happy` — the path behaves as a human expects.
`sharp` — the path meets a constraint, a limit, a version floor, or a staleness.

### D4 — attendance (2 positions)

**is a human present to read what the system says.**

| position | who reads the output | the real paths |
|---|---|---|
| `attended` | a human, at a terminal, now | a developer runs `rhx enroll`; a driver walks a route |
| `unattended` | **nobody** | a CI enroll · a provisioned host · a fleet sweep · a clone that drives its own route |

**it is orthogonal to D2, not a function of it.** every moment can occur unattended: an enroll can
run in CI (`bind`), a provisioned clone can boot (`deliver`), a fleet sweep can rewrite a manifest
(`change`), and an unattended long-lived clone compacts (`survive`). ⇒ the wish is framed around a
**fleet**, so the unattended half is the half that scales.

#### 🟡 it is DEGENERATE on `happy` — declared, never assumed

on a happy path the system produces the outcome and says so; **whether a human read the message
does not change the outcome.** checked against all 8 happy cells individually — in each, the
attended and unattended runs are byte-identical in effect.

⇒ so the axis **collapses on `happy`** and is **live on every `sharp` cell**, and the reason is one
sentence:

> **every sharp cell's remedy in this design is a HUMAN-READABLE SIGNAL — a warn, a halt message,
> a refusal that names the fix. "fail loudly" is meaningless with no listener.**

so the unattended half of each sharp cell asks a question the attended half never has to: **what
does "loud" mean when nobody reads stdout?** the answer must be *machine*-detectable — a non-zero
exit code, a structured log line, a written marker file — and **the blueprint owes that contract.**

### D5 — enrollment (2 positions)

**did the clone come up through `rhx enroll`, or was the binary launched bare.**

| position | how the clone started | what it has |
|---|---|---|
| `enrolled` | `rhx enroll claude …` — rhachet owns the spawn | the corpus, an address, a socket |
| `bare` | a human typed `claude`; a CI job called the binary directly | **no role corpus, no address, no socket — but the REPO DEFAULT arrives (S13)** |

**F1's verdict is what makes this axis carry weight**: the role corpus lives in the **actor's** dir,
reached by a `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` only the enroll sets, so a launch that bypasses the spawn carries no
role briefs. a walk that assumes the transport ran cannot express the case (`.seeds/…case=S7`).

🟡 **the mechanism changed twice and the axis did not.** under S6 the corpus rode a **spawn flag**;
under S12 it rides a **spawn env var**. either way a bare launch misses it ⇒ **the axis is
transport-independent**, which is why F1's reversal left it upright.

**S13 narrowed the `bare` position and did not collapse it.** `rhx init` writes
`<repo>/.claude/AGENTS.md`, which the ancestor walk finds with **no** env var and no enrollment ⇒ a
bare clone now gets the **repo default**. it still lacks its actor's roles and is still
unaddressable, so both cells stand — but the loss is **partial**, and a partial loss is **harder to
catch** than a total one (`case=9`).

#### it is orthogonal in principle, and it COLLAPSES HARD — declared, never assumed

`bare` is not a variant of a cell; **it is the absence of the machinery every other cell presumes.**
checked against each axis rather than asserted:

| axis | what `bare` does to it |
|---|---|
| **D1 corpus** | collapses. neither corpus arrives, and for one reason — no flag, no hook registration |
| **D2 moment** | collapses. there is no `bind` (naught was rendered for this process), no `change` (the clone is unaddressable, so no route can reach it), and `survive` preserves an empty set |
| **D3 feel** | collapses to `sharp`. there is no happy `bare` path — the state is a defect by S7 |
| **D4 attendance** | **stays live**, as it does on every sharp cell. the remedy is a refusal, and a refusal owes a reader |

⇒ **16 sub-positions collapse to 2.** the collapse is by *nature* — the mechanism produces one
experience regardless of the other axes — which is why it is recorded here as a property rather
than left to the reader to notice.

🟡 **and this axis does not touch the other 24 cells**, which are all `enrolled` by construction.
it **adds** two cells; it does not multiply the extant space.

## .the product

```
enrolled:  2 (corpus) × 4 (moment) × 2 (feel) × 2 (attendance) = 32 positions
             ├─ 16 happy   → attendance degenerate → collapse pairwise →  8 cells
             └─ 16 sharp   → attendance live       → stay distinct     → 16 cells
                                                                   ⇒ 24 cells

bare:      16 sub-positions → D1/D2/D3 all collapse by nature      ⇒  2 cells
                                                                   ⇒ 26 total
```

every one of the 26 is verdicted in the inventory — **none is omitted**, since a census that skips
a cell cannot report its own gaps. the 8 collapsed happy cells and the 14 collapsed `bare`
sub-positions each carry the collapse as their recorded reason, so the omission is *declared*
rather than silent.

## .the axes considered and REJECTED

stated so the walk is auditable, and so a later reader does not re-derive them:

| rejected axis | why it is not a dimension |
|---|---|
| ~~**actor** {human, clone}~~ | **split, and half of it was promoted.** *is a human present to read the output* is real and orthogonal ⇒ **D4 `attendance`**. what remains — *who performs the act* — genuinely is determined by D2, and that residue stays rejected |
| **transport** {system-prompt-file, rules/, CLAUDE.md, hook, …} | **a solution, not an experience — and the space is not fully stable across it.** the honest statement: **the CELL SET is stable across transport, and two cells' VERDICTS are not.** #7/#8 were marked `TRANSPORT-BOUND` and owed a re-walk (`rule.require.experience-catalog-evolution`). ⚠️ **that obligation was declared DISCHARGED by S6 and F1's REVERSAL (S12) has FIRED it** — the new transport is a layer-2 file **re-read from disk**, the exact branch the mark was placed for. ✅ **the re-walk was performed and both cells KEPT their verdicts**: #7 survives compaction because the file's content is unchanged rather than because the prompt was recorded; #8 stays `criti` on the **hash** form, where a role change is a different path. **the rejection is VINDICATED by the reversal, not weakened by it** — a transport axis would have multiplied 24 cells by 4 candidates and would *still* have needed this re-walk, since the answer turned on an actor identity form that no transport axis names |
| **role count** {one, many} | **a parameter, not a dimension.** it changes the corpus size, which is already captured by `sharp` at the budget/cap boundary |
| **brain** {claude, opencode, …} | **out of the wish's bounded scope.** the wish names the `claude-cli` floor. carried as a dream, not a cell |
| **concurrency** {solo, overlapped} | **barred by NATURE, and F1's REVERSAL (S12) BROKE TWO OF THE THREE BARS.** three experiences were checked, one per race direction, and the record is kept rather than rewritten so the loss is legible. (1) *reader × reader* — two live sessions on one role set: the bar was *"each cli process holds its own recorded system prompt"*. ⚠️ **gone** — a re-read transport gives both a live handle on one file. **what replaces it**: the corpus is a pure function of `{ brain, roles }`, so with no writer in play both reads return identical bytes, whenever they happen. (2) *writer × reader* — an enroll rewrites the artifact while a session holds it: the bar was *"the reader already recorded its copy"*. ⚠️ **gone**, and on a **slug** or **derived** actor this race is now **REAL** ⇒ `dimensions.md` invariant 4 is **downgraded to open**, and the hazard is **divergence, not corruption** (temp+rename still bars a half-read). on a **hash** actor it stays barred, since a rewrite of a different roleset lands at a different path. (3) *writer × writer* — two `rhx enroll` runs on one artifact path: ✅ **UNTOUCHED, and it never rested on record-once** — identical content by construction, plus **temp-write + rename**, atomic on POSIX (`genBrainCliConfigArtifact.ts:90-100` — *"can never leave the brain-cli to read a half-written settings file at boot"*). ⇒ **the axis is STILL rejected, and one of its three reasons is now weaker rather than absent**: direction 2's residue on a slug actor is not a new cell — it is `role × change` and `role × survive`, already walked, already sharp, and now carried as `[research]` rows 1 and 1′ |
| **density** {sparse, dense} | **not an axis at all — an evaluation lens on a DEMO**, per `define.experience._.metric=boundary-density`: *"density is not a third axis of a path."* it does not partition the space; it ranks which walks through the space deserve the richest demo. ⇒ it lives as a **column in the case inventory**, never as a dimension here |

🟡 **the density row is the one rejection a reader is most likely to re-open**, because density
genuinely changed the demo set — it is why `case=7` exists. it changed *which cells were walked
together*, never *which cells exist*. that is exactly the line between a lens and an axis.

## .the invariants the walk surfaced

a dimensional walk earns its completeness by what it declares **cannot** occur:

**F1 REVERSED (S12) and the contingency these carried has FIRED.** invariants 1, 2, and 4 each
rested on a **recorded** system prompt. the transport is now `<brainDir>/AGENTS.md` — a layer-2
file **re-read from disk** — so record-once no longer holds them up. each is re-stated below with
what now carries it, and 1 and 4 are **downgraded from invariant to open**.

1. ⚠️ **`role × change` cannot be served in-session — DOWNGRADED to open.** the record-once
   argument is gone. what remains is weaker and form-dependent: on a **hash** actor a role change
   is a *different path*, so this session's file is byte-unchanged forever and the cell stays hard
   — and the hash form is what `rhx enroll` produces, so the fleet's default is unaffected. on a
   **slug** or **derived** actor the path holds and the content is rewritten, so a re-read *would*
   serve it. ⇒ it is now an **invariant on the hash form only**, and a measurement elsewhere
   (`[research]` row 1). ⇒ demonstrated as `case=6`.
2. ✅ **`adhoc` can never occupy the cached prefix — HOLDS, on the prefix argument alone.** its
   record-once argument is gone; invariant 3's *"an append cannot bust a prefix"* is its converse
   and still carries it. a mid-route rewrite of a prefix file is cache-hostile whether or not it is
   inert, and a re-read that lands at an unpredictable moment is worse than one that never lands.
   🟡 **it lost a reason, not its truth** — and it is now single-stranded.
3. ✅ **an append cannot bust a prefix — HOLDS, untouched.** it never rested on record-once. so
   `adhoc × change × happy` is achievable *by construction*, whatever transport the blueprint picks
   for it. ⇒ demonstrated as `case=5`. it is also, after the reversal, the one that carries #2.
4. ⚠️ **two live sessions cannot race on one corpus — DOWNGRADED to open.** it said each cli
   process *"records its own system prompt … and never re-reads the file"*. a re-read transport
   voids the second clause outright, so two live sessions on a **slug** actor could observe a
   concurrent write at different moments. the extant temp-then-rename guard means neither reads a
   half-written file, so the hazard is **divergence, not corruption**. ⇒ a measurement
   (`[research]` rows 1 and 1′), and the reason the corpus-identity record survives the reversal.
5. ⚠️ **an unenrolled clone cannot receive either corpus — AMENDED by S13, and it still HOLDS.**
   the role corpus rides a spawn **env var** (a flag under S6; the amendment is cosmetic) and the
   adhoc corpus rides a hook registered in the enroll-generated settings — so a launch that bypasses
   `rhx enroll` bypasses both, by one mechanism rather than two. ⇒ this is what collapses D5's `bare`
   half to two cells, and it is a **nature** bar on every other axis at once.
   **what S13 adds is a THIRD corpus the invariant never named**: `<repo>/.claude/AGENTS.md`, on
   the ancestor walk, which a bare clone **does** receive. the invariant is scoped to *either
   corpus* — the actor's role corpus and the route's adhoc corpus — and neither reaches a bare
   launch, so the sentence is true as written. ⚠️ **it is now easy to misread as "a bare clone gets
   naught"**, which S13 made false, so the scope is stated here rather than left to the reader.
