# 2.2.criteria.blackbox.matrix.yield — the coverage matrices

> **six matrices, none past three independent dimensions.** each distills one related set of
> usecases from `2.1.criteria.blackbox.yield.md`, walks the full product, and marks every cell
> either with its outcome or as a **GAP**.

## .how to read a row

- **ind:** an input that varies — a condition a test sets up
- **dep:** an outcome the criteria promise — what a test asserts
- **🔴 GAP** — a combination whose outcome the criteria do NOT specify. each is named below its
  matrix, with what would close it
- **⊗ collapsed** — a combination that cannot vary, with the reason declared rather than assumed

🟡 **a collapse is declared, never silent.** the census's own rule: *"a census that omits a cell
cannot report its own gaps."* where a dimension is degenerate under a condition, the row says so.

## matrix 1 — the role corpus arrives at boot

⇒ usecases **1, 2, 3** · cells #1 #2 #3 #4 #17 #18

**the collapse, declared:** where the cli is **below the floor**, the enroll refuses *before* any
corpus is rendered ⇒ `corpus.render` cannot vary. its 3 positions collapse to 1, on both
attendances. **6 positions → 2 cells.**

| ind: cli.version | ind: corpus.render | ind: attendance | dep: enroll | dep: clone.spawned | dep: briefs.present | dep: exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| below floor | ⊗ any | attended | **refuses** — names the **resolved path** it found, every other `claude` on PATH with its version, which one **wins PATH**, and the repair command | **no** | — | **non-zero** |
| below floor | ⊗ any | unattended | **refuses**, identically — the whole diagnosis is in the error, never in a tty | **no** | — | **non-zero** |
| at/above | whole | attended | succeeds | yes | **all** | 0 |
| at/above | whole | unattended | succeeds | yes | **all** | 0 |
| at/above | oversized | attended | succeeds | yes | **all** — no truncation | 0 |
| at/above | oversized | unattended | succeeds | yes | **all** — no truncation | 0 |
| at/above | absent/corrupt | attended | **fails LOUD** | **no** | — | **non-zero** |
| at/above | absent/corrupt | unattended | **fails LOUD** | **no** | — | **non-zero** |

**the symmetry that matters:** `attended` and `unattended` produce **identical** dep values in
every row. that is the design goal, not an accident — the census showed a printed-only remedy
evaporates without a reader, so **every outcome here is machine-detectable**.

🔴 **rows 1–2 carry the vision's `case=3` `[t0]`/`[t1]` cells in full.** an earlier draft of this
matrix read only *"names the repair command"*, which dropped them: the measured failure is not
*"no cli"* but **two installs, and the wrong one wins PATH** — so an error that says only
*"upgrade claude"* sends a human to upgrade the install that was already fine. the refusal names
the file it found, what shadows it, and which one PATH picks.

⚠️ **rows 5–6 are the wish's whole motive.** today an oversized corpus is silently truncated; the
criteria promise `delivered == rendered`, so `oversized` and `whole` must read identically.

🔴 **no gaps.**

## matrix 2 — an actor's roles change

⇒ usecases **4, 10** · cells #5 #6 #19

| ind: change.kind | ind: actor.form | ind: when.observed | dep: corpus.updated | dep: act.owed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| role added to actor | hash | same session | **no** — impossible by mechanism | re-enroll, stated plainly |
| role added to actor | hash | next clone | **yes** — a new hash, a new actor | none |
| role added to actor | slug/derived | same session | 🔴 **GAP** | 🔴 **GAP** |
| role added to actor | slug/derived | next clone | **yes** — re-derived at spawn | none |
| package upgraded | hash | same session | **no** | re-enroll |
| package upgraded | hash | next clone | **yes, if the actor is ACTIVE** | none |
| package upgraded | slug/derived | same session | 🔴 **GAP** | 🔴 **GAP** |
| package upgraded | slug/derived | next clone | **yes, if ACTIVE** | none |

### 🔴 gap 1 — the slug/derived actor, same session

the criteria specify the **hash** form: its path is content-addressed, so a role change lands at a
*different* dir and this session's corpus is byte-unchanged forever. **a slug actor holds its
path** and its corpus is rewritten in place — so a mid-session re-read *could* deliver the change,
and whether it does is unmeasured.

⇒ **closes on `[research]` 1.** `rhx enroll` produces hash actors, so the specified rows cover the
fleet's default; the gap is real for `actors.yml`-declared actors.

### ⚠️ the `ACTIVE` predicate is undefined

rows 6 and 8 promise a re-render for an **ACTIVE** actor and the word has no definition — an actor
has no socket, so the clone-liveness term does not transfer. **not a gap in the matrix** (the
outcome is specified); **a gap in the vocabulary**, and a blueprint deliverable.

## matrix 3 — a session crosses a boundary

⇒ usecase **5** · cells #7 #8 #16 #20 #24

| ind: corpus.kind | ind: boundary | ind: role.set.changed | dep: present.after | dep: detection | dep: exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| role | compaction | no | **yes** | — | 0 |
| role | compaction | yes | **stale** | signal fires | ⚠️ open |
| role | resume | no | **yes** | — | 0 |
| role | resume | yes | **stale** | **comparison fires** | **non-zero** |
| adhoc | compaction | no | **yes** — re-delivered | — | 0 |
| adhoc | compaction | yes | **yes** — re-delivered | — | 0 |
| adhoc | resume | no | **yes** — re-delivered | — | 0 |
| adhoc | resume | yes | **yes** — re-delivered | — | 0 |

**the asymmetry is what this matrix surfaces.** the **adhoc** half is insensitive to
`role.set.changed` — a route's briefs are re-bound at the next stone regardless — so its four rows
collapse to one outcome. the **role** half is not, and that is where every critipath sits.

⚠️ **row 2's `exit` is open, deliberately.** the criteria state the **comparison** fires; whether
it halts or warns at the compaction boundary is `[research]` 1. row 4 (resume) is specified as
non-zero, because the unattended resume is cell #20 and it has exactly one live boundary.

🔴 **no gaps** — one open value, named.

## matrix 4 — the adhoc corpus is bound

⇒ usecases **6, 7** · cells #10 #11 #12 #13 #15 #21 #22

| ind: manifest.state | ind: attendance | dep: halt | dep: briefs.present | dep: cached.prefix | dep: exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| within budget | attended | no | **yes** | **UNDISTURBED** | 0 |
| within budget | unattended | no | **yes** | **UNDISTURBED** | 0 |
| over budget | attended | **at author time**, names remedies | — | — | **non-zero** |
| over budget | unattended | **at author time**, machine-readable overage | — | — | **non-zero** |
| names naught | attended | **fails LOUD** | — | — | **non-zero** |
| names naught | unattended | **fails LOUD** | — | — | **non-zero** |

**`UNDISTURBED` is the load the whole cache argument carries.** a route advance that invalidated
the prefix would void the feature's benefit on every long route.

⚠️ **the `over budget` halt is at AUTHOR time, never at drive time** — the overage is caught by
the person who can fix it, before a clone is driven. that moment choice is what makes rows 3–4
identical in `exit`.

🟡 **decomposition signal — see the note at the end.** this matrix's subject is the peer branch's
budget gate, not this behavior's transport.

🔴 **no gaps.**

## matrix 5 — the render is deterministic

⇒ usecase **9** · the S15 requirement

| ind: package.version | ind: location | dep: bytes.identical |
|---|---|---|
| same | same worktree, twice | **yes** |
| same | two worktrees, one repo | **yes** |
| same | two machines | **yes** |
| upgraded | same worktree | **MAY differ** |
| upgraded | two worktrees, one repo | **MAY differ** |
| upgraded | two machines | **MAY differ** |

**the `upgraded` rows are the bound, and without them the requirement is untestable.** an
unbounded determinism claim names no two renders to compare. ⇒ `location` is degenerate under
`upgraded` — all three rows say the same — **and the rows are kept rather than collapsed**,
because the symmetry is what shows the bound is about *version* alone.

🔴 **no gaps.**

## .the gap register

| # | gap | what closes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | matrix 2 — a **slug/derived** actor's role change, same session | `[research]` 1: does a slug actor's corpus rewrite in place, and does a mid-session re-read deliver it? |
| 2 | matrix 3 row 2 — halt **or** warn at the compaction boundary | `[research]` 1, same measurement |
| 3 | matrix 2 — the **`ACTIVE`** predicate has no definition | a blueprint deliverable, not a measurement |
| 4 | matrix 6 rows 3–4 — the bare clone's fail-safe | the enrollment-mandate behavior (S8), then a re-walk |

⇒ **gaps 1 and 2 close on one measurement.** gap 3 is a vocabulary debt. gap 4 is out of bounds by
a wisher's scope call, with its bar recorded.

## 🟡 .decomposition opportunities

**matrix 4 belongs to a different behavior.** its subject is the **adhoc** corpus — a route
manifest, its budget gate, its author-time halt — and that gate is the **peer branch's**
(`beav/feat-boot-manifest-and-budget`), not this one's. this behavior's subject is the **role**
corpus and its transport.

| what stays here | what belongs to the peer |
|---|---|
| matrices 1, 2, 3, 5 — the role corpus: render, bind, deliver, survive, determinism | matrix 4 rows 3–6 — the budget gate and the manifest lookup |
| matrix 3's **adhoc** half — that adhoc survives a compaction | matrix 4 rows 1–2's `UNDISTURBED` — a joint contract the two must agree on |

⇒ **the seam is clean because the two behaviors meet at one assertion**: a route advance leaves the
cached prefix undisturbed. that is a contract, and a contract is exactly where two behaviors should
touch.

⚠️ **matrix 6 is likewise a different behavior** (the enrollment mandate, S8) and is already marked
deferred. it is kept here only to hold its bar.

⇒ **no matrix exceeds three independent dimensions, and none needs a split for size.** the two
split signals above are about **ownership**, not about explosion.
