# 3.1.1.research.external.product.flagged._ — what the cli does with a brain dir

> **the repo's brain dir is reached by the `agents-md` walk. the actor's brain dir is NOT — that walk
> has no user scope. so the actor's brain dir needs a `CLAUDE.md`, and the vision's fallback clause
> is met by the record, not by a test.**

quotes and source titles are verbatim, as the stone requires. each claim carries a label —
`[FACT]` · `[SUMP]` · `[KHUE]` · `[OPIN]` — and its sources, numbered in `.bibliography` with date and
credibility tag.

the vision's open measurements (`1.vision.appendix=groundwork.md`) and the premises the transport
rests on:
