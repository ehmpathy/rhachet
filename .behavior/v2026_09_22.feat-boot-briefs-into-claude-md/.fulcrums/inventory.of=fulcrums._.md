## .how to read a status

| status | means |
|---|---|
| **RULED** | the wisher decided; cited seed or words |
| **TAKEN** | the driver decided; the wisher may overrule |
| **RESOLVED** | a measurement or a later design removed the fork's premise |
| **SUPERSEDED** | a later verdict covers it; a narrower successor may be open |
| **VOID** | the subject of the fork no longer exists |
| **ACCEPTED** | contested by a reviewer, then passed |
| **DISPUTED** | a reviewer's premise was refuted by a measurement; the driver holds, the wisher may overrule |
