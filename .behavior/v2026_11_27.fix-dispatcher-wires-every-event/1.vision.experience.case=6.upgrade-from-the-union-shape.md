# case=6 — upgrade from the union shape: the union wire leaves, one `*` wire lands

`human × rhx upgrade × E=union` · happy / sharp · critipath

## .narrative

a human upgrades rhachet on a repo synced months ago. long ago they hand-added a `Bash` formatter
hook to their settings, and they still rely on it.

they run `rhx upgrade`. the swap report names four dispatchers, one per event. they open
`settings.of.repo.json` to check: each event holds one rhachet dispatcher under `*`, and their own
formatter hook is still there, under the matcher it always had. they run a `Bash` call: the
formatter runs once, the role guards run once. naught ran twice, naught went dark.

the fear they might hold — that the upgrade would fold their hand hook into rhachet's, or drop it —
does not come true. rhachet only moves what rhachet wrote.

## .the mechanism, for the reader who asks

the sync drops every rhachet dispatcher hook it finds (`isHookReplacedByDispatch`), wherever its
matcher sits, then writes one dispatcher per mapped event under `*`.

.note = a shared entry arises on the clone-derive path, where `asSettingsWithHookDispatchers` drops
hooks one by one (`asSettingsWithHookDispatchers.ts:25-38`). the repo sync writes through the brain
adapter, which keys each entry by an `# author=` matcher tag
(`genBrainHooksAdapterForClaudeCode.ts:150`), so there a hand-added hook never shares the
dispatcher's entry. [t0] is a test of the derive path; the adapter path is clamped by its own
`setHookDispatchersViaAdapter` integration test.

## .[tn] sketch

```
given('settings with a union-matcher PreToolUse entry that holds the dispatcher and a hand-added hook')
  when('[t0] the sync runs')
    then('PreToolUse holds a `*` entry with the dispatcher, once')
    then('the union entry holds the hand-added hook alone')
  when('[t1] the sync runs again')
    then('the settings are byte-equal to [t0]')
```
