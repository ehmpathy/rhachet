# case=6 · the cap holds

## .narrative

the human unlocked with the default 9h. at `+8h55m` the grant nears expiry, but the cap is `+9h`.
the renew may set the expiry to `+9h` at most. after `+9h` no renew fires and the key is gone,
exactly as it would be today. the `--duration` the human chose still means what it says
(fulcrum F1).

c1's overnight walk sits inside the default 9h cap until the portal lapse at `+8h`, so in the
default case the portal ends first and the cap never bites.

## .timeline

```
given('[case1] an sso grant whose unlock cap is near')
  when('[t0] the renew tick runs')
    then('the renewed expiry never exceeds the cap')
  when('[t1] the cap passes')
    then('no renew fires and the key expires')
```
