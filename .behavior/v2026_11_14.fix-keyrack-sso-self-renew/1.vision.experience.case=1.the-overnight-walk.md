# case=1 · the overnight walk

the densest walk: six boundaries, two actors, one night. it is also the wish's live proof
("ahbode.camp.AWS_PROFILE passes two expiries with no hand unlock").

times read as offsets from the unlock (`+0`).

## .narrative

early in the day (`+0`) the human unlocks the camp key and leaves. grove 0901's tunnel reads it on
each reconnect. at `+55m` the grant enters the renew window; the daemon proves the portal session
silently and pushes the expiry to `+1h55m`. it does so again at `+1h50m`, `+2h45m`, and on.

the laptop sleeps from `+4h` to `+4h40m`, through the `+4h30m` expiry. on wake the next tick finds
the grant past its expiry but under its cap, and renews it. the tunnel's next reconnect works.

at `+8h` the portal session ends. at the `+8h20m` renew window the silent proof answers "lapsed"
within seconds; no browser opens. the daemon marks the key lapsed and stops to retry. the grant
lives until `+8h30m`, then expires; the tunnel's reconnect now fails.

the next day the human runs `keyrack status`, sees `lapsed` and the cure, runs it, and logs in in
the browser. the fresh grant replaces the lapse mark, and renews resume.

## .timeline

```
given('[case1] the camp key unlocked at +0, default cap, portal live until +8h')
  when('[t0] the grant enters its renew window at +55m')
    then('the daemon renews it silently; expiresAt moves to +1h55m')          # renew boundary
    then('the tunnel keyrack get returns the profile with no gap')
  when('[t1] the laptop sleeps +4h to +4h40m, past the +4h30m expiry')
    then('the first tick after wake renews it; keyrack get returns it again')  # past-expiry boundary
  when('[t2] keyrack status runs at +6h')
    then('the key shows renewed = 6x')                                         # observe boundary
  when('[t3] the portal session ends at +8h and the +8h20m window opens')
    then('the renew fails within seconds, no browser login')                   # lapse boundary
    then('the key is marked lapsed')
  when('[t4] the next ticks run')
    then('no renew attempt fires again')                                       # no-retry boundary
  when('[t5] the human runs the cure from status')
    then('the fresh grant replaces the lapse mark; renews resume')             # cure boundary
```
