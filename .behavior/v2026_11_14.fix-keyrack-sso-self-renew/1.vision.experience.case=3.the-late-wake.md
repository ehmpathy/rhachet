# case=3 · the late wake

## .narrative

the human closes the laptop and opens it 40 minutes later. the camp key expired 30 minutes into the
sleep, and the tunnel's reconnect 5 minutes after that never ran, since the host slept too. a minute
after wake the tunnel's next reconnect calls `keyrack get`, and the key is there: the first tick after wake
already renewed it. the human sees naught amiss; `keyrack status` shows the key with
`expires in: 59m` and its renew count one higher.

why it works: the store purges an expired grant on read, so the tick does not walk the store. it
walks its own record of sso grants, which still knows the key and its cap, and renews it from there.

## .timeline

```
given('[case1] an sso grant past its expiry after a sleep, portal live, cap ahead')
  when('[t0] the first renew tick after wake runs')
    then('the grant is renewed from the renew record')
    then('keyrack get returns it again')
    then('keyrack status shows the renew count one higher')
```
