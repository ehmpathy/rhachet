# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

> the axes an enroll's keybindings link varies on, and the walked product.

## .dimensions

| # | dimension | positions |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | source `~/.claude/keybindings.json` | `file` (or a symlink to a file, e.g. a dotfiles repo) · `absent` (no entry, a symlink whose target is absent, or a `~/.claude` that is itself a file: stat follows links and folds ENOENT and ENOTDIR into absence) · `not-a-file` (a dir, or a symlink to one) · `unreadable` (a link loop, ELOOP; or a file this process may not read, EACCES / EPERM, mode 000 included) |
| D2 | prior entry at `<brainDir>/keybindings.json` (the top name) | `none` · `our-arrow` (→ `.home/.claude/keybindings.json`) · `dead-arrow` (another target, absent) · `foreign-arrow` (another target, present) · `real-file` · `dir` |
| D2h | prior entry at `<brainDir>/.home/.claude/keybindings.json` (the home name) | `none` · `our-arrow` (→ `<home>/.claude/keybindings.json`, present or not) · `foreign-arrow` · `real-file` · `dir` |
| D3 | entry point | `enroll` (`invokeEnroll.ts:788`) · `init`, repo actor (`setRepoActorBrainDirLayout.ts:47`) · `init`, derived-actor sync over each active actor (`setActorSettingsDerived.ts:44`) |
| D4 | actor form | `repo` (tracked dir, reached via `<repo>/.claude`) · `hash` · `slug` · `derived` |
| D5 | clone state | `fresh spawn` · `live clone, source edited` · `live clone, source created after spawn` · `live clone, source removed after spawn` |
| D6 | concurrency | `solo` · `two findserts that overlap on one brain dir` · `a clone reads while a findsert swaps` |

## .orthogonality of the kept axes

- **D1 ⟂ D2 ⟂ D2h.** the source lives in the human's home; the two prior entries live in the brain
  dir. no source state constrains what a prior run, a clone, or a hand left at either name, and the
  two names are written apart (a hand can leave a file at one and not the other). so every
  combination can occur
- every combination is covered by two faces, D1 × D2 and D1 × D2h, each walked cell by cell. the
  findsert decides each name by its own rule, and neither decision reads the other name's state, so
  a D1 × D2 × D2h cube cell is the pair of its two face cells and adds no outcome of its own

## .the axes that collapse, and why

- **D3 is not a fork.** all three entry points call one findsert (`setBrainDirSettingsLayout`) with
  the real home (`getHomeDir()`), so a cell holds for each. the derived-actor sync is how a `derived`
  (and every other active) actor's brain dir gains the arrows at `rhx init`; an enroll of that actor
  lays them too. walked once
- **D4 is not a fork.** one layout for every actor (`define.brain-dir-layout-is-one-for-every-actor`).
  the one actor-form difference is git: only the repo actor's dir is tracked, and its `.gitignore`
  is an allowlist (`/*` first), so both names are ignored with no edit. walked once, with a git check
  on `repo`
- **D5 rides D1 × D2 over time.** `source edited` is `file × our-arrow`: a symlink, so the edit is seen
  at the next read. `source created after spawn` is `absent × none` at spawn: no arrow until the next
  findsert (`rhx init` or an enroll). `source removed after spawn` is `absent × our-arrow` at the next
  findsert: our arrows go; until then the clone reads ENOENT and falls back to defaults. each time
  state is its own inventory row
- **D6 rides every cell.** the findsert holds the brain dir's `settings.of.repo.json` mutex for its
  whole span (`withRepoMutex`), so two runs that overlap serialize: the second meets the first's
  result, which is the `our-arrow` column. a reader mid-swap meets the old entry or the new arrow,
  never ENOENT, since `setArrow` swaps by one `rename(2)` (`setSymlinkSwapped`). each is its own row

- **D1 `unreadable` writes as `absent`.** the findsert lays no arrow and removes only rhachet's own
  entries, as for `absent`; its rows differ in the `UNLINKED` reason (`keybindings unreadable`) and in
  one `ℹ` line enroll prints, since a human who holds a file expects it to load

⇒ the walked product: **D1 × D2 = 4 × 6 = 24 cells** and **D1 × D2h = 4 × 5 = 20 cells**, plus
**3 time rows**, **2 concurrency rows**, and **1 notice row**, each verdicted in
`1.vision.experience.case=_.md`.
