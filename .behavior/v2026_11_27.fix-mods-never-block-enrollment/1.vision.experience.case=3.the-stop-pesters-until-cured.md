# case=3 — the clone's stop pesters until the fault is cured

## .narrative

the clone works a whole session on a skipped driver mod. each time it stops, the repo-wide stop
sweep (`roles cost --all --when hook.onStop`, already armed by any mod dir) names every mod fault
with its fix and holds the stop, as it holds for a moved pin today. the clone either cures it
(when the fix is in its reach) or tells its human. once the fix lands, the next stop says naught.

the sweep rides the mods dir, never a role the seat holds: a seat with only package roles is the
seat that most needs the pester, since it may never boot the faulty role at all. held by
`getLinkedRolesWithHooks.integration.test.ts` case8 and `enroll.mods.integration.test.ts` case13 [t1].

the pester is per turn, never per stop: a stop claude already held passes, so an interactive clone
whose fault needs its human is never trapped in a stop loop. held by `enroll.mods.integration.test.ts`

`hooks.dispatch.acceptance.test.ts` [t9b] and `constants.manifest-parity.integration.test.ts` [t4].

## .why the stop reads the mod, never the record

the stop derives each fault from the mod as it stands — its spec, its plan, the hook check, the
role dir, the mods path. it never reads the refusal the last render recorded, and never asks the
boot gate: after a cure, the gate names the view stale (`assertRoleModdedViewFresh.ts:113-118`)
until a re-render, and a stale view of a sound mod is no fault to hold a stop on. so a cure the
clone applies mid-session clears the next stop, before any re-enroll; the next enroll renders it.
