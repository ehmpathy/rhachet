# case=2 — a mod dir with no boot.yml

## .narrative

the commander drafted a driver mod in a hurry: he dropped `briefs/x.md` into
`.agent/repo=.mods/role=driver/` and never wrote its `boot.yml`. at the start of the next day his
resume of the director seat exits 2, and he loses the first hour to a hunt for why.

with the fix, the same resume lands. the clone opens and says, in its own boot, that the driver
mod is skipped because it holds no `boot.yml` — and gives him the one line to add. he adds it when
the seat is idle, re-enrolls, and the driver boots modded.

## .bdd sketch

```
given role=driver linked, and .agent/repo=.mods/role=driver/briefs/x.md with no boot.yml (C1)
  when [t0] rhx enroll claude --resume=director
    then it exits 0
    then boot.md's driver section holds <mod.skipped of="driver"> with
      the mod of role=driver is skipped: it holds no boot.yml
      fix: add .agent/repo=.mods/role=driver/boot.yml with `of: <repo>/driver`
    then boot.md holds no line of briefs/x.md
  when [t1] boot.yml is added with of: rhachet-roles-bhrain/driver, and the commander re-enrolls
    then it exits 0, the driver boots modded, and no <mod.skipped> remains
```
