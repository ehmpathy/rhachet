# case=8 — two faulty mods in one walk

## .narrative

the commander keeps two mods: one for the driver, one for the mechanic. in one afternoon he breaks
both — a typo'd note path in the driver mod (C4) and an orphan `.md.min` in the mechanic mod (C5).
today his resume exits 2 on whichever the sync reaches first, he fixes that one, and the next
resume exits 2 on the other. two faults cost him two lost resumes.

with the fix, one resume lands and names both. each mod is judged alone: the driver section and
the mechanic section each carry their own `<mod.skipped>`, with their own fix, and the readout
names both at once. the clone's stop names both under one header, so the two cures can land in one
pass. a cure of one clears its own notice and leaves the other.

## .bdd sketch

```
given role=driver and role=mechanic linked and enrolled
  and .agent/repo=.mods/role=driver/briefs/notes.md, which no say or ref names (C4)
  and .agent/repo=.mods/role=mechanic/briefs/x.md.min, with no briefs/x.md beside it (C5)
  when [t0] rhx enroll claude --resume=director
    then it exits 0
    then stderr names 2 mods skipped: role=driver with its C4 fix, role=mechanic with its C5 fix
    then the driver section of boot.md holds <mod.skipped of="driver"> with the C4 fix, and no line of briefs/notes.md
    then the mechanic section of boot.md holds <mod.skipped of="mechanic"> with the C5 fix, and no line of briefs/x.md.min
  when [t1] the stop hook runs
    then it exits 2
    then stderr holds one header, 2 mod faults, held until cured
      and one branch per role, each with its own problem and fix
  when [t2] only the driver mod is cured, and the stop hook runs again
    then it exits 2, under a header of 1 mod skipped, and names role=mechanic alone
  when [t3] the mechanic mod is cured too, and the stop hook runs again
    then it exits 0 and prints naught
  when [t4] rhx enroll claude
    then it exits 0, both roles boot modded, and no <mod.skipped> remains
```
