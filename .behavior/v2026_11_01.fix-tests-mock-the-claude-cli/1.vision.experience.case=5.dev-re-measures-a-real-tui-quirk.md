## .narrative

a dev suspects the real claude tui now draws its queue hint two rows lower, which would trip the
mock's `busy` screen out of step with reality. today they would re-run
`clone.screen-dogfood.realbrain.acceptance.test.ts` — a jest suite that runs on every pr and, beside
its clamps, spends haiku turns to emit research grids no gate reads.

after: the measurement is a byhand skill, in the shape `claude.cli.secstore.trial` already takes. the
dev runs it on demand against a real claude on haiku. it prints what the real tui drew. if the
shape moved, the dev updates the mock's mode to match, and the per-pr suites go on to exercise the
new shape for free.

sharp half: an author who tries to keep the measurement as a jest suite meets the fence (case 3) —
a real-claude spawn from a suite outside the smoketest set is refused. the refusal names the skill
as the home for a measurement.
