## .settled

**review budget is earned by a live `urgent` concession, and by no other cause.** an all-`better`
round at the budget floor is *good enough* — the driver spends the floor and lets the point evolve.

⇒ the law already held it, in three places at once:

| the rule | what it says |
|---|---|
| `define.invariant.review.peer.budget.urgent-earns-budget` | a budget hit needs more budget ⟺ a live `urgent` concession stands |
| `rule.always.concede-with-a-severity` | *"a `better` concession that earns budget = **blocker**"* · *"`better` never earns budget. spend the floor and evolve"* |
| `rule.require.grade-a-concession-by-its-harm` (reviewer) | *"a `better` concession cited as a reason to increase the budget = **blocker**"* |

🔴 **so this seed records no new law. it records a MECHANISM of violation**, and that is the durable
part:

> **the guard PRINTED the budget command, and the print was read as a warrant.**

on refusal of an absorb at the floor, the halt reads *"fix the nitpick, buy the round, then
re-arrive"* and names `route.guard.budget --add 2`. the driver had already reasoned the rule
correctly two turns earlier — *"all concessions are `better`, none `urgent`, so no human is owed"* —
and then spent the lever because the halt offered it.

⇒ 🔴 **a printed lever is not a warrant.** `rule.always.spend-own-levers-before-escalation` says
budget is driver-owned and *"not scarce"* — and OWNED is not the same as PERMITTED. the identical
distinction `rule.always.raise-a-blocker-a-taken-cannot-close` draws about the commit quota: *"it got
wrong that human-gated implies worth a human's grant."* here: **driver-owned implies neither
warranted nor free.**

🟡 **the second-order cost, which is why the rule exists:** the budget is the maintenance floor. to
buy a round for a `better` point is to stop the floor in order to bound aught — it spends the
mechanism that exists to force convergence, on a point that ships no harm.
