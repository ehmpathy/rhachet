## .settled

**the sweep is human-callable. that was never the open question.**

⇒ 🔴 **the drive's question was malformed, and that is the part worth the seed.** it posed
*"human-callable, or hook-only?"* as a fork with two live arms. there is one arm:

| a gate a human **can** type | a gate a human **cannot** type |
|---|---|
| they can inspect it before they trust it | they must trust a verdict they cannot reproduce |
| they can run it mid-work, on demand | they wait for a hook to fire |
| its render is reviewable | its render is seen only in the moment it fires |

**a mechanism that refuses work owes its subject a way to ask it in advance.** an inspectable gate
is not a feature to weigh against hook-only; it is a property a gate has or lacks.

🟡 **and the inverse is what actually varies** — see `S14`. *"may a human type it?"* has one answer;
*"may a hook fire it unprompted?"* is the real fork, and it resolved the other way.
