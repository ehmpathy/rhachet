## 🔴 .why — the rule is already written, and it grades this a BLOCKER

`rule.prefer.emoji-language` carries a section titled *"rhachet has no voice — mascots belong to
roles"*:

| the rule says | so |
|---|---|
| rhachet is the framework, not a character | its generic cli speaks in the **neutral** slots only |
| `🐢` is the **mechanic's** mascot, from a supplier package | it is not this repo's vocabulary at all |
| 🔴 *"a rhachet-generic line that wears a role-mascot"* | 🔴 **= blocker** |

⇒ **the halt was a blocker by a rule that sat in the booted context the whole time.** `roles boot` is
rhachet-generic cli; the seaturtle belongs to `rhachet-roles-ehmpathy`.

## 🔴 .and `🐙` would have been wrong too — which is the instructive half

the obvious repair is to swap one mascot for another: rhachet's registry mascot is `🐙` the octopus
(`define.mascots-and-artifacts`, enroller). **that is also forbidden**, and the reason parts two
concepts a single word had blurred:

| the artifact | what it carries a mascot for |
|---|---|
| a **role registry**'s readme + its roles' own skills | ✅ `🐙` — the registry's character |
| 🔴 rhachet's **own generic cli** | 🔴 **no mascot.** the framework has no voice |

⇒ so *"which mascot?"* was the wrong question. **the right question was which SLOT**, and the answer
is `domain-root` — a slot whose whole purpose is to say which tool speaks, with no character at all.

🟡 `rule.require.keyrack-emoji-palette` had already litigated this exact case one domain over:
keyrack roots on `🔐` and *"every keyrack output roots directly on `🔐 {command}` — no
`🐢 bummer dude…` blocked vibe header."* **the precedent was one brief away and I did not reach for
it.**

## 🔴 .the arrears it surfaced — two glyphs in use, neither registered

the repair needed a boot-domain glyph, so the catalog was read rather than a glyph coined
(`im_an.obsessive_learner.for.domain.glyphs`: *a grep proves a glyph UNUSED, never UNCLAIMED*). the
read found the opposite defect — **glyphs in use with no row**:

| glyph | in use at | registered? |
|---|---|---|
| `🧢` role | `syncHooksForLinkedRoles.ts:174` | 🔴 **no** |
| `🥾` boot | `invokeBriefsBoot.sh:3` | 🔴 **no** |

⇒ both rows added to `rule.prefer.emoji-language` this round. **the arrears is what the round found;
the mascot was only the trigger.**

🟡 and the two are not redundant: `🧢` roots a surface that NAMES a role, `🥾` roots the boot act
where no role is named — a `--manifest` render, `hook.onBoot`.

## ✅ .what the case files already had right

**8 of the 9 sites were already conformant trees** — `🧢` root, `✋ over budget`, treestruct
branches. the defect was a **stray vibe header bolted above** them, which is precisely the shape the
keyrack rule names. ⇒ the repair was a deletion at 6 sites and a re-root at 3, never a redesign.
