## 🔴 .the declared gap — this inventory opens at S1, and the route did not

⚠️ **every wisher utterance that predates S1 is unarchived.** each was distilled straight into an
artifact with no seed behind it — *"rhx, no adhoc npx tsx / entool this as an rhx skill durably"*
among them. ⇒ **the gap is not in what this inventory holds; it is in where it starts.** the
seventeen seeds above are complete.

🟡 **not repaired retroactively, deliberately.** a seed's `.said` is verbatim in substance; to
reconstruct an utterance from its distillate would invert the whole point — the seed exists to check
the distillate, so a seed *derived from* the distillate proves naught.

🔴 **the durable check, for the next route: create `.seeds/` the moment the route is bound, empty.**
the rate above is the argument — **5 of the 17 seeds amended, rewrote, or removed a requirement**, and an
inventory that starts with the route catches the early utterances at the same rate. an empty
inventory that declares its axis costs a minute and cannot go stale; a late one loses material that
cannot be honestly reconstructed.
