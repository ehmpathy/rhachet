## .the narrative

Bo's repo links four upstream role packages and owns two roles of its own. a boot halts, and the
halt names `roles cost --all` — the one command that hands him the cost half of the choice the halt
leaves him. he types it.

```
🧢 6 boot specs — 4 budgeted, 2 over budget
   ├─ 4,476 / 5,000 tokens (90%)  .agent/repo=.this/role=any/boot.yml
   ├─ 269 / 5,000 tokens (5%)  .agent/repo=.this/role=resident/boot.yml
   ├─ 18,235 / 12,000 tokens (152%)  .agent/repo=bhrain/role=driver/boot.yml (linked) ⚠️
   ├─ 496 / 20 tokens (2480%)  .agent/repo=linked-repo/role=lodger/boot.yml (linked) ⚠️
   │
   └─ swept
      ├─ .agent/repo=*/role=*/boot.yml
      ├─ .behavior/*/boot.yml
      ├─ .route/*/boot.yml
      └─ src/domain.roles/*/boot.yml

   .note = a spec outside these paths is not swept. name it directly to cost it —
           `roles cost --what <path>`.

   .note = a `(linked)` spec is owned by another repo — not yours to trim. its budget is
           that repo's to raise, so `roles boot` warns on it rather than halts. upgrade
           the package, or raise it upstream. (2 of 6 here)
```

**both over-budget rows are marked, and neither is his.** he stops reaching for a trim he cannot
make, upgrades one package, files an issue upstream on the other, and turns back to the two rows
that are his. **elapsed: he never opened a file he could not write.**

## 🔴 .the defect this closes — a hazard requirement 8 sealed, reopened one surface over

| grain | who owns the spec | what the reader is told |
|---|---|---|
| the **gate** (`roles boot`) | mixed | ✅ **a foreign spec WARNS** — requirement 8. the caller is never held to a file they cannot write |
| the **report** (`roles cost --all`) | mixed — 🔴 **the same population** | 🔴 **was: not one word.** every over-budget row read as the reader's to fix |

⇒ the sweep inherited the exact population requirement 8 was written for, and rendered it flat.
**13 of this repo's own 15 specs are a symlink into a version-pinned store**, so the majority of a
real roster is rows no reader of it can act on.

🔴 **and the report is the WORSE surface to lose it on.** a gate fires at a moment the reader did
not choose; a report is typed on purpose, by a reader who came for exactly this arithmetic. *"halt
with no remedy the caller can act on"* costs more when the reader sought the page out.

---

## 🔴 .why the tag is on the PATH, never on the verdict

the row already carries a verdict marker — `⚠️`, for over budget. the ownership tag is a second,
orthogonal fact, and the two must not fuse:

| the mark | what it asserts | what varies it |
|---|---|---|
| `⚠️` | this spec is **over its cap** | the arithmetic |
| `(linked)` | this spec is **another repo's file** | one `lstat` |

⇒ so `(linked)` sits beside the **path** it describes, and `⚠️` stays at the end where a scanner's
eye already looks for a verdict. a merged glyph would make *"over budget"* and *"not yours"* one
condition, and they are independent — a linked spec **under** its cap is still not yours.

🟡 **it is words rather than a glyph, deliberately.** a new glyph owes a catalog row and a sense
the reader must learn before the first read pays off (`rule.prefer.emoji-language` — a glyph is
claimed, never coined mid-render). `(linked)` reads on contact.

## .the bdd

```gherkin
given a repo with one OWN spec under its cap and one LINKED spec over its cap
  when [t0] `roles cost --all`

  then it exits 0
    # a report reports, whoever owns the specs. the halt lives at `roles boot`

  then it costs BOTH specs — the own one and the linked one
    # ownership decides the TAG, never the population. a sweep that skipped foreign
    # specs would answer a question the reader did not ask

  then the LINKED row carries `(linked)`
    # the assertion the case exists for

  then the OWN row does NOT
    # the half a uniform fixture cannot grade. a hard-coded tag passes every other
    # assertion and fails this one

  then the roster says what the tag MEANS, and what to do
    # "owned by another repo" · "not yours to trim" · "warns on it rather than halts".
    # a marker with no sense is a puzzle (`rule.require.errors-name-the-fix`)

  then the whole roster matches its snapshot — on STDOUT
```

⇒ clamped at `blackbox/cli/roles.cost.acceptance.test.ts` `[case12]`, against a fixture whose link
is a **real `roles link`** rather than a `symlinkSync` stand-in — `roles link` is the mechanism of
foreign ownership, not a signal of it
(`define.invariant.a-symlink-under-agent-is-foreign`).

🔴 **teeth proven by revert**: with the tag forced off, 2 of the 6 assertions go red — the tag
assertion and the snapshot. with it forced on, the *own-row* assertion goes red instead. **the pair
brackets the predicate from both sides**, so neither a constant `true` nor a constant `false`
survives.

## 🟡 .what this case does NOT claim

it does **not** claim the sweep should refuse a foreign over-budget spec. `S13` settled that a
report refuses naught, and `S14` struck the one gate that would have. the tag changes **what the
reader knows**, and changes the exit code not at all.

nor does it re-open `G2`. a foreign spec still **warns** at the boot gate and is still an itemized
alterpath there — because at the *gate* the reader is mid-task and the warn is the whole message.
**the report is a different surface with a different reader**, which is why it earns its own cells
rather than a note under `G`.
