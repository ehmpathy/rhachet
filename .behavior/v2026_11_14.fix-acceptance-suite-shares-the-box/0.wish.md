the rhachet acceptance suite is a good neighbor on a shared box, and still fast:

- on a laptop or a grove, the suite takes a bounded share of the cores, so claude seats, the desktop, and peer crews keep their cpu
- on ci, the suite keeps its full width
- a run leaves no fixture dirs behind in the temp dir
- the suite is no slower on ci than it is today, and as fast as it can be on a shared box
