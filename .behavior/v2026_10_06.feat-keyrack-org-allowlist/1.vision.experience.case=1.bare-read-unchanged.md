# case=1 — the bare read is unchanged 🟢 criti

## .narrative

kai maintains `ahbode/svc-jobs`. its manifest says `org: ahbode`, as it has for a year. he never
hears about the allowlist. he runs `rhx keyrack unlock --owner ehmpath --env prep` at dawn, then
`get --key DB_PASSWORD`. both do what they did yesterday, to the byte.

next door, `uladkasach/dev-env-setup` adds `aether` to its list. its bare reads still land on
`ahbode` — the first entry. the tests, the hooks, the `git.commit.set --push` token: all of them
read `ahbode.*` exactly as before. the new seat is invisible until someone names it.
