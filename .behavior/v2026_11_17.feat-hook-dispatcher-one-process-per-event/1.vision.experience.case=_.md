> the coverage contract. every cell of the walked product (`1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`),
> verdicted once. demoed cells link to their `case=N`. rows never overlap: a row names the exact
> rule kinds (axis C) it covers, so each (B, C, D) cell sits in one row.

## .the densest walk

`case=9.a-seat-session-end-to-end` crosses seven boundaries in one session: SessionStart context with
an `rhx-init` frame, an all-allow call, a one-block, the `ask` json beside `rhx` stdout headers, an
`rhx-skill` frame, a many-block, and a Stop refusal beside two foreign rules. it is the centered
demo; the cases below are its spotlights, one per sharp critipath, so each fail-safe stays legible.
