# case=10 — a peer clone without the driver role

`human × enroll --roles -driver × {fresh}` → `seat × Stop / PreToolUse` · sharp · critipath

## .narrative

a human wants a second opinion while their driver works a route, and enrolls a peer in the same
worktree: `rhx enroll claude --as @:peer1 --roles -driver`. the repo has measured what happens if
the peer carries the driver's hooks: it boots the route and edits the files the driver holds open
(`rule.forbid.peer-clones-that-carry-the-driver-role`). the human asks the peer a question and
expects only an answer.

today a clone's settings keep only inner hooks whose `author` the clone enrolls
(`getSettingsForRoles.ts:13-45`). with the dispatcher, the clone's settings hold one dispatcher
hook per event, so the cut must live in the clone actor's own manifest: no `repo=bhrain/role=driver`
row. the human gets the answer they asked for; the peer's Stop does not refuse with a stone, and
the driver's files stay the driver's.

## .[tn] sketch

```
given('a repo actor with driver enrolled, and a peer actor derived with --roles -driver')
  when('[t0] the peer actor's hooks are synced')
    then('its settings hold one dispatcher hook per event')
    then('its manifest holds no row authored by repo=bhrain/role=driver')
  when('[t1] the peer's Stop is dispatched')
    then('route.drive does not run; the stop is not refused by a stone')
  when('[t2] the repo actor's Stop is dispatched')
    then('route.drive runs and refuses, as today')
```
