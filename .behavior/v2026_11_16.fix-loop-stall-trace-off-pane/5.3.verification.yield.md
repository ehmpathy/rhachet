### criteria changed, on purpose

one acceptance row is inverted, not dropped. `enroll.reach` [case8] once held a row, "the RAW trace
line above the frame is locked too", that pinned `clone socket ready rejected with …` on the human's
screen, ahead of the `💥` frame. the wish's `.done means` (`0.wish.md`) requires the opposite: no
daemon trace line reaches a pane's tty. the row's own docblock said it locked a cost, never a
blessed shape. two rows replace it:

- the screen holds no daemon line and no trace stamp — the same broad read as [case4]
- the same line lands in the repo's daemon trace log, named as a bind fault, its words unchanged

the vision that set this was approved by the wisher before the route reached execution; the
`.note` on the row cites the wish. the wisher ruled it to stand, 2026-11-17 (fulcrum F11).

one hint grew, and kept its advice: the wedged `clone say` hint still says the brain may be busy,
retry, and re-enroll if it stays unresponsive; it adds where the daemon trace lives. no other prior
assertion was removed or weakened.
