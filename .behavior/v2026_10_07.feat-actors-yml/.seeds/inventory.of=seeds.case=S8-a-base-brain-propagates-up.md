# S8 — a base's brain propagates up

## .said

> base actor contributes brain too. it just propagates up

## .settled

a base actor contributes its brain as well as its roles. the brain propagates up the `@ref` chain to every actor that extends it, at any depth, and a local `brain:` overrides it. an actor with no base must state `brain:`; two `@ref` bases with different brains and no local `brain:` is ambiguous and fails validation.
