# S5 — a brain or role delta beside an actor spawns a delta clone

## .said

> you can mix it with brain and roles delta optionally, to override -> create delta clone

## .settled

the actor form admits overrides. beside `@<slug>`, a brain and `+role` / `-role` deltas apply on
top of the actor, and the result is a delta clone: a derived actor `<slug>._.delta=<hash>`,
tethered to its base, so an edit to the base still reaches it. the actor stays the source of
brain and roles; an override is a named variation of it, never an anonymous mix.
