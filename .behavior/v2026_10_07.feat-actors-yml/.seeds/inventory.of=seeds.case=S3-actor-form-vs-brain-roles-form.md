# S3 — when to name an actor vs a brain and roles

## .said

> and relevant syntax of when to use `--actor` vs `--brain --roles`

## .settled

an invocation names its subject in one of two forms, never both:

- **the actor form** — `[--actor] @<slug>`: a declared actor, which supplies its brain and roles
- **the inline form** — `[--brain] <brain> [--roles] <roles>`: an undeclared composition, spelled out

the vision states which form fits which need, and what happens when both appear.
