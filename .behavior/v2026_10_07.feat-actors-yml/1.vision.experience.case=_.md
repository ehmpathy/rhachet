# 1.vision.experience.case=_ — the inventory

every cell of the reduced product in `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`, verdicted.

- **feel** = happy · sharp
- **care** = critipath · alterpath (care = freq × cost)
- **verdict** = demoed · itemized · forbidden · impossible
- **cell** = one position per axis D1–D7. `any` = the verdict holds for every position of that axis;
  `{a, b}` = it holds for each listed position; `—` = the axis has no position in that cell. D8 and D9
  default to `declared` and `bare` unless the row names them. D3's two invalid positions — `ambient:` absent (row 11)
  or a typo (row 12) — are D1=`invalid` cells; they keep their D3 position so the row names the key at
  fault. every other row with a manifest is D3=`declared`, since `ambient:` is required by name (S9)
