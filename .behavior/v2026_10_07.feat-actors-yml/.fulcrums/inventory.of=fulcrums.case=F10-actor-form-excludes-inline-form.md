- **fork** — (a) `@<slug>` and a brain / `--roles` never mix; a tweak to an actor rides as positional deltas (`@scholar -driver`) · (b) a brain or `--roles` beside `@<slug>` overrides the actor's (`rhx enroll @scholar codex`)

- **confidence** — 75% — a human who wants "scholar, but on codex, once" must now write a yml entry or spell scholar's roles inline; (b) is cheaper in that one case
- **where** — contract row *invocation*; inventory rows 33, 40; case=8 `[t2]`
