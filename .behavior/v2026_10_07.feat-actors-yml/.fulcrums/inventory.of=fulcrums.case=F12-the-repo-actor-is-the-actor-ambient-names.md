# F12 — under a manifest, the repo actor IS the actor `ambient:` names

- **fork** — main (d91e8270, #600) made the repo actor `actor.via.repo=ambient`, the one actor that holds the ambient roles, with `<repo>/.claude` linked to its brain dir. under a manifest: (a) the declared actor becomes the repo actor — a top-level `ambient: '@<slug>'` names it, the ambient roles are its roles, and `<repo>/.claude` plus a bare enroll reach `actor.via.slug=<slug>` · (b) keep `actor.via.repo=ambient` as its own dir and render the declared actor's roles into it — a twin of `actor.via.slug=<slug>` · (c) keep the key `default:` and map it onto the repo actor
- **taken** — (a). main's invariant is *ambient roles live only in the repo actor*, and main already collapses an enroll whose roles equal the ambient set into that one actor rather than a twin. (b) would hold two dirs for one `{ brain, roles }`, which `rule.forbid.per-clone-config` names as drift. (c) reintroduces "default", which main retired on both axes (`term=ambient`); the repo actor is the one that holds the ambient roles. this absorbs F5
- **rework** — clean — the key name is one schema field, and the dir choice is one resolver: which actor dir `<repo>/.claude` points at
- **confidence** — 65% at the time — the key name was a guess (`repo:` was drafted first); and a repo that adopts a manifest sees its bare clones change dirs once, at the next init, which a council may prefer to avoid via (b)
- **where** — contract rows *shape*, *repo actor*, *clone identity*; Q3; inventory rows 4, 10–12, 29; case=4
- **verdict** — **ruled (a)** by the wisher (S9), with the key settled: a top-level `ambient: '@<slug>'`, not `repo:`. it is required in every manifest and names its actor by name — no sole-actor fallback, so a file without it is invalid in every reader
