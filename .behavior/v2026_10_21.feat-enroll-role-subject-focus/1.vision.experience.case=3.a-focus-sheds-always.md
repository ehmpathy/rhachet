# case=3 — a focus sheds `always`

cells: **P3**, **K1** (the yml that leads it), **K2** (`[t3]`, the misspelled key refused), **SK1**
(`[t0]`, the subject's own skill lands), **S2D** (`[t1]`, two foci with mixed opt-out). feel happy ×
care criti (P3, K1, SK1), happy × alter (S2D), sharp (K2).

## .narrative

mechanic's `always` block holds the tone, term and prose briefs, plus every mechanic skill, about
19k. a `101` focus meant to be small would carry all of it by default.

so the role author declares on the subject that it does not inherit `always`:

```yaml
subject.101:
  inherit:
    always: false
  briefs:
    say:
      - briefs/practices/code.prod/pitofsuccess.errors/rule.require.failfast.md
      - briefs/practices/code.prod/pitofsuccess.errors/rule.forbid.failhide.md.pt1.md
  skills:
    say:
      - skills/git.repo.test/git.repo.test.sh
```

the author ships it, and the commander enrolls the director seat at `mechanic::101`. the receipt
says the focus shed `always`, so when the clone later finds no `git.commit.set`, the commander
already knows why, and knows the remedy is a second focus that inherits. the seat now fits its
budget with two rules and one test skill, and every rail still fires on the clone's tool calls.

the commander who also wants flow adds `mechanic::flow` and watches `always` come back: flow
inherits it, and `always` is shed only when every chosen subject of the role opts out.

## .the timeline — a sketch

```
given('[case3] mechanic boot.yml declares subject.101 with inherit.always = false')
  when('[t0] rhx enroll claude --roles any,mechanic::101')
    then('boot.md holds subject.101 briefs and no always brief or skill')
    then('boot.md lists git.repo.test — a subject`s own skills land at its focus')
    then('every mechanic hook is still present')
    then('the receipt says mechanic::101 shed always — so a commander who expected git.commit.set learns why it is absent')
  when('[t1] rhx enroll claude --roles any,mechanic::101,mechanic::flow   (flow inherits)')
    then('boot.md holds always + 101 + flow — always sheds only when every selected subject opts out')
  when('[t2] rhx roles boot --repo ehmpathy --role mechanic   (no subject)')
    then('the render is byte-identical to today — the flag reads only under a focus')

given('[case3.b] the role author misspells the key: subject.101 holds inherits: { always: false }')
  when('[t3] rhx enroll claude --roles any,mechanic::101')
    then('exit 2, ConstraintError — a key in subject.101 carries an always field, but the opt-out key is `inherit`')
    then('no actor dir is written — the author never ships a "small" focus that silently carries always (K2)')
```
