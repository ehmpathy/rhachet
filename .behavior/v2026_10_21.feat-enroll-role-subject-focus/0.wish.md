# wish: enroll a role at a subject focus — `mechanic::101`

## the need

a director seat drops role=mechanic to fit its boot budget (mechanic boots 65.1k). but enroll
narrows hooks to the enrolled roles, so the seat also loses mechanic's ~15 safety hooks:
check-permissions, the term checks, cross-repo access, tmp writes, stderr redirect, shell syntax,
planmode, and the rest. the commander wants the seat to keep the hooks and a small set of basic
code briefs, without the full role.

boot.yml already declares subjects (`subject.code.prod`, `subject.flow`, ...), and
`rhachet roles boot` and `roles cost` take `--subject`. `rhachet enroll --roles` and
`rhachet init --roles` take no subject.

## what done means

1. `--roles` on `enroll` and `init` accepts `<role>::<subject>`, e.g. `--roles any,director,mechanic::101`,
   in the exact-set and the delta forms (`+mechanic::101`).
2. a role enrolled at a subject boots only that subject's briefs and skills, and keeps ALL of
   the role's hooks. hooks follow the role, never the subject.
3. a subject may declare that it does not inherit `always`, so a small focus is not forced to
   carry the role's whole `always` block (mechanic's is ~19k).
4. `roles cost --all` and the boot-budget check count the subject, not the whole role.
5. an unknown subject fails loud and names the role's declared subjects.

## bounds

- no change to how a role with no focus boots.

## why now

the commander's direction, 2026-10-21: director seats boot without mechanic, and must not lose
its rails. the copy of mechanic's hooks into role=director is held while this lands.
