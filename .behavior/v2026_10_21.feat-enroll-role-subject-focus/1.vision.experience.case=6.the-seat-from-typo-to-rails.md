# case=6 — the seat, from typo to rails

cell: **J**, the journey. feel sharp → happy × care criti. three actors (commander, clone,
auditor), four boundaries crossed as steps: the parser's refusal, the boot render, the hook on a
tool call, and the cost sweep. the hook sync is a given here; `case=1` `[t1]` crosses it.

## .narrative

the commander bakes the director seat in one sit-down session.

1. **the typo** (parser → selection). they type `mechanic::flo`. the enroll halts, names flow
   among mechanic's subjects, and writes no actor dir
2. **the fix** (selection → render). `mechanic::flow` lands. the receipt shows the focus. the
   seat's `boot.md` holds `always` and flow, plus one line that names the subjects left out
3. **the rail** (render → hook sync). the clone, mid-task, reaches for `rm -rf build/`. mechanic's
   PreToolUse hook blocks it and points at `rmsafe`. the rail came with the focus
4. **the read** (hook sync → cost sweep). the auditor runs `roles cost --all` and sees
   `mechanic::flow` priced at its focus, not at mechanic's 65.1k, so no one is pushed to drop
   mechanic again

the journey proves the wish's claim end to end: no step trades a rail for a token.
