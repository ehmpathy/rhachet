# case=2 — the focus refuses loud

cells: **P4**, **P5**, **P12**, **S1**. feel sharp × care criti (P4, P5) / alter (P12, S1).

## .narrative

the commander types `mechanic::101` a week before rhachet-roles-ehmpathy ships `subject.101`. the
enroll halts before it writes a byte. the refusal names the role, the subject that was typed, and
the subjects mechanic does declare, which is the extant shape `roles boot --subject` already gives
(`computeBootPlan.integration.test.ts:568-606`). the commander picks `flow`, and the second try
lands.

the same halt guards the three other mistakes a commander can type: a focus on a role with no
subjects, a focus on a subtraction, and one role named both whole and focused.

## .the fail-safe proof

every refusal fires at parse or at selection, before the actor dir, the boot.md, or the settings
file is touched. so a typo costs one re-run and leaves no state behind.
