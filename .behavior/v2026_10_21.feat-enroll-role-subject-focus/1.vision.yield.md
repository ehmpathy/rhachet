# 1.vision — enroll a role at a subject focus

## .the outcome world

### before

a director seat cannot afford mechanic's 65.1k boot, so it enrolls without mechanic. enroll
narrows hooks to the enrolled roles (`getSettingsForRoles.ts:16-64`), so the seat also loses
mechanic's ~15 PreToolUse rails: check-permissions, the term checks, cross-repo access, tmp
writes, stderr redirect, shell syntax, planmode. the stopgap on the table is to copy mechanic's
hooks into role=director, a second copy that drifts from the first.

### after

```sh
rhx enroll claude --roles any,director,mechanic::101
```

the seat boots mechanic's `101` subject alone, a handful of code briefs, and keeps every one of
mechanic's hooks. one token draws one line: **hooks follow the role; briefs follow the focus.**

### the aha

the commander no longer trades safety for budget. under this change, a role carries two parts:
its rails, which are cheap and always worth their cost, and its knowledge, which costs real tokens
and is often only partly needed. before, the two came as one unit. now the seat takes the rails
whole and only the slice of knowledge it needs. the copy of mechanic's hooks into director is
never written.

## .the contract

| surface | the proposal |
|---|---|
| **the token** | `<role>::<subject>` — also `<repo>/<role>::<subject>`. `::` is free: no role path splits on `:` today (`parseRoleSpecifier.ts:22` splits on `/` only) |
| **where** | every `--roles` form on `enroll` and `init`: exact `mechanic::flow`, add `+mechanic::flow`. an exact set may name several foci of one role, which union (S2). a `+` delta keeps one entry per role and upserts it, so `+mechanic::flow` atop a held whole `mechanic` narrows it, atop a held `mechanic::code.prod` moves it, and the receipt names the change (`F6`). `prepare:rhachet` is patched the same way. `upgrade --roles` names packages, so a focus there is refused with the package form as the fix (`F7`); upgrade re-renders each held focus as it stands |
| **the boot** | the role renders with `--subject <subject>` semantics: `always` + the subject, no `<also>` roster, plus one line that names the subjects not given, so the clone knows the rest exists. the leaf already takes per-role subjects (`getOneRoleBootContent.ts:32`); `setBrainDirBoot.ts:41` hard-codes `null` today |
| **the hooks** | the focus is stripped before the hook filter. every hook the role declares lands, the same set a bare enroll writes |
| **`always` opt-out** | a subject section may declare `inherit: { always: false }`. `always` is shed only when every selected subject of that role opts out; the receipt says so. a misspelled key that carries an `always` field refuses at parse rather than be dropped in silence |
| **identity** | the focus is part of the roleset. for a hash actor (`rhx enroll`), `mechanic::flow` and `mechanic` are two actors with two hashes (`genEnrollmentHash.ts:25`). for a slug or derived actor, the roleset is edited in place and the path holds, as for any role change (`catalog.of=actor-clone-design`, invariant 3) |
| **the cost** | `roles cost --all` gains, under each subject-mode spec, a row per declared subject, priced at the payload that focus would boot, beside the whole-role row. the sweep stays keyed by spec and reads no actor (`F4`) |
| **refusals** | an unknown subject, a focus on a role with no subjects, a focus on a subtraction, and a role named both whole and focused: each halts at exit 2 before any write |

## .mental model

- **to a friend:** *"you can enroll just part of a role now: its guardrails, plus one topic of
  its docs."*
- **the analogy:** a contractor's badge and a contractor's manual. the badge (hooks) gets you
  through every checkpoint. the manual (briefs) is many chapters, and you only carry the
  chapter for today's job. you never get the chapter without the badge.
- **their words vs ours:** a commander says "mechanic, but just the 101 stuff". we say role,
  subject, focus. `subject` is the boot.yml key; `focus` names a role enrolled at a subject. it is
  the wish's own word, and the receipt the commander reads should use it.

## .evaluation

| goal | how well |
|---|---|
| keep the rails without the full role | fully. the hooks and the briefs are selected independently |
| a small focus | only with the `always` opt-out. mechanic's `always` is ~19k on its own, so without the opt-out a "small" focus is still about 19k plus the subject |
| a cost that tells the truth | it needs the new rows, one per declared subject. today's sweep reads mechanic at 65.1k, which would push the commander to drop it again |
| no change to an unfocused role | held. the bare-role path renders byte-identically (`case=1` `[t2]`) |

**pros:** one token, no new flag. it reuses the subject sections that already exist, the extant
`--subject` selection, and the extant unknown-subject refusal (`computeBootPlan.ts:268-276`). it
removes the need to copy hooks into director.

**cons:** identity forks per focus, so a change to a seat's focus makes a new actor rather than
an edit to the old one. `101` does not exist yet: mechanic declares `code.prod`, `code.test`,
`arch` and `flow` (`mechanic/boot.yml:99,218,257,267`). and with `always` shed, the seat also
loses the mechanic skills that live under `always`, git.commit.set among them.

**pit of success:** every misuse halts before the first write and names the fix. the one
silent failure we found (D1, the drop on re-derive) is promoted to a critipath with its own demo.

🔴 that holds at the type level only. at least six sites compare a slug to a linked role by exact
string, and each would read `mechanic::flow` as an unknown role: the enroll check
(`computeBrainCliEnrollment.ts:97-127`), the incremental merge (`:69-91`), the boot-ref map
(`asRoleRefsForEnrolledSlugs.ts:22-24`), the re-derive (`asActorRolesRederived.ts:25-27`), the hook
filter (`getSettingsForRoles.ts:44-45`) and the prepare patch (`asPrepareRhachetRolesDelta.ts:58-70`).
the vision does not prescribe how; it asserts that each site must see the role and the focus apart.

## .what is awkward

- **`101` is a promise, not a subject.** the wish's headline example cannot run until
  rhachet-roles-ehmpathy declares it. the feature is real today with `flow`, but the seat that
  prompted the wish waits on a second repo
- **`always` opt-out also sheds the skills.** mechanic puts every skill under `always`, so a
  `101` that opts out boots no `git.commit.set`. that may be exactly right for a director, or a
  surprise. the subject's author decides, and the receipt should show what was shed
- **a focus change is a new actor, for a hash actor.** this is correct by the sync invariant, but
  a commander who bakes seats with `rhx enroll` may expect an edit to the seat's focus to keep the
  seat. a slug actor keeps its path, as it does for any role change
- **two places to configure, one of them upstream.** the token lives in this repo, while the
  opt-out and the `101` subject live in the role's boot.yml. a commander who wants a smaller
  focus must go change a role they may not own
