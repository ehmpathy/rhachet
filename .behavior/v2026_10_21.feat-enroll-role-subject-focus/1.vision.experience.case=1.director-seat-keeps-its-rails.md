## .narrative

the commander bakes a director seat. mechanic's full boot is 65.1k, too large for the seat, so
before this change the seat dropped mechanic and lost its ~15 PreToolUse rails with it.

now the commander types one more token: `mechanic::flow`. the receipt lists mechanic with its
focus beside it. the seat's `boot.md` holds the flow briefs, plus mechanic's `always` block,
and no code.prod or code.test briefs. it carries no `<also>` roster of the rest either: a
focused boot omits what it did not select (`RoleBootSpec.ts:166`).

the clone is told what it was not given: one line names mechanic's other subjects, so a clone that
needs test rules knows they exist and that the seat was narrowed on purpose. it costs one line,
never their briefs.

later the clone types `grep -rn foo src/`, and mechanic's check-permissions hook blocks it, just as
it would in a full mechanic seat. the rails came along, while most of the briefs stayed home.

🟡 `flow` stands in for `101`. mechanic declares `code.prod`, `code.test`, `arch` and `flow`, and
no `101` today. `101` is a subject rhachet-roles-ehmpathy must add (see the yield).
