# 1.vision.experience.case=_ — the inventory

the coverage contract. every cell of the collapsed walk carries a verdict; every critipath carries a
demo. the axes and the collapse arguments live in `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`.

feel: **happy** / **sharp**. care: **criti** / **alter** (care = freq × cost).

## .the primary walk — B × C × D, after the collapse

A is not a column. `enroll` and `init` share the parse, the refusals and the selection, so each row
holds for both; their persistence differs, and each side's path has its own cell (D1, S3, P7).
`upgrade` takes no roleset, so its cells are U1–U3 below. the demos cover `enroll` (`case=1`,
`case=2`, `case=3`) and `init` (`case=4`).

care = freq × cost. within an exact/add twin the cost is the same, and the freq is not: the wish's
director seat is enrolled by an exact set (`--roles any,director,mechanic::101`), so the exact form
is the high-freq path and carries the criti grade. the add form is criti only where it owns a step
the exact form lacks — the merge into a held set (P7, and E1–E4 below).

## .the forbidden cells — each a rejection invariant

| # | the attempt | why refused |
|---|---|---|
| P12 | `-mechanic::flow` | a subtraction removes a role from the set. a focus on it names a partial removal the set has no shape for — is `mechanic` left at "every subject but flow"? refuse, and name the fix: `-mechanic` removes the role whole; to change its focus, re-enroll with the exact set. 🟡 fulcrum `F1` |
| S1 | `mechanic,mechanic::flow` | the bare role already boots every subject, so the focus is either redundant or a contradiction. refuse rather than guess which was meant. 🟡 fulcrum `F2`. the refusal reads the tokens the commander typed in one exact set; a `+` delta atop an ambient whole role is S3, an upsert, never this |
| V2 | a focused boot with no roster line | nurture: a focused boot always writes the one line that names the subjects not given. without it the clone cannot tell "this role has more" from "this is all of it", and would improvise what another subject of the role already paves |
| U2 | `rhx upgrade --roles mechanic::flow` | upgrade's `--roles` names packages to upgrade, and a package has no focus. refuse at exit 2, and name the fix: `rhx upgrade --roles mechanic` upgrades the package, and every actor focused on it re-renders at its focus (U1, U3). 🟡 fulcrum `F7` |
| K2 | a key in a subject section, other than `inherit`, whose value carries an `always` field | the section schema is non-strict, so the key would parse and be dropped, and the focus would carry `always` in silence: the role author believes it is small when it is not. refuse at parse and name `inherit` as the key expected. the test is structural, the twin of `assertNoInertBudget`'s by-name branch (`parseRoleBootYaml.ts:52-61`), never a list of typos |

## .the rank — by boundary density

0. **J** — the journey crosses the parser's refusal, the boot render, a hook on a tool call and the
   cost sweep in one walk, across three actors. the densest cell, so it is centered
1. **P2 + H1** — the wish's whole point: the seat boots small and keeps every rail. it crosses the
   parser, the boot render, and the hook sync, and the two halves pull in opposite directions
2. **P4 / P5** — the typo and the wrong-role case. the first a commander hits
3. **P3** — the `always` opt-out; without it a small focus still carries mechanic's ~19k `always`
4. **R1 / R2** — the auditor's read; a budget check that counts the whole role defeats the feature
5. **D1** — the silent drop on re-derive; low frequency, high cost (a seat loses its rails unseen)
6. **P1** — the no-regression floor; widest blast radius, every extant enroll
