## .settled

**budget is the lever for a reviewer that RAN and needs another round. it is not the lever for a
reviewer that cannot run at all.**

the two look identical on the guard's tree — both render a fraction — and they are opposite
conditions:

| the lane | what more budget buys |
|---|---|
| **rejected**, rounds left | a chance to confirm your repair. spend it |
| **constraint / malfunction** — it could not run | naught. the fraction was never the limit |

⚠️ **a terminal lane does NOT hold a higher level.** the guard says so in its own words —
*"terminal — does not block higher levels"* — so a lower level stuck on a constraint is not a wall
between the drive and l3. to keep it funded is to spend the human's attention on a lane whose
verdict was never about the artifact.

⇒ read the VERDICT before the fraction. a lane that spent its round to raise a blocker wants
budget; a lane that overflowed its context wants a scoped re-run
(`rule.always.rerun-dark-review-lanes-scoped`), and neither wants a top-up.
