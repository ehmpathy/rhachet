# 1.vision — workers spawn, reviewers are leaves

> **governed by the ehmpathy/rhachet#577 vision.** depth is a recorded fact of the chain, and the
> bound on fan-out is the **leaf mark**.

## .the rule, in one table

the bound is on **how a clone was spawned**, never on its depth and never on its roles.

- **a work clone may spawn** one level below itself, at any depth
- **a reviewer clone is a leaf** and may spawn no one. a print-mode enroll (`rhx enroll … -p`)
  and every repl-door child (`rhx review`, `rhachet ask`, `rhachet act`) is a reviewer; the
  enroll or repl call that mints it sets `RHACHET_CLONE_LEAF=1` in the child env (F23)
- **depth is a fact, never a limit.** a tty with no depth var is depth 0; every other spawn is
  caller depth + 1 (F6, F13). it is carried to every child so the chain tells the truth

| who | depth | leaf? | may spawn? | e.g. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a top-level clone — a human starts it at a tty, via `rhx enroll` or a raw `claude` | 0 | no | ✅ yes | its guard enrolls reviewers; it runs `rhx review` |
| a worker a top-level clone enrolled in session mode | 1 | no | ✅ **yes** — the #577 case | a storefront worker that runs its own stone's reviews |
| a worker's worker | 2+ | no | ✅ yes | a fan-out a driver chose to build |
| a reviewer — a `-p` enroll's child, or a repl brain | any | **yes** | ❌ no — refused loudly, the reviewer rule named | a guard's reviewer that tries `rhx enroll` or `rhx review` |

the bound has two named edges:
- **an interactive enroll mints a work clone**, even where a human types *"review this"* into it.
  only the door marks a reviewer (F23)
- **atoms pass.** an atom brain is one api request with no process and no tools, so it spawns no
  clone and carries no mark (Q2)

⚠️ a work clone that is refused is the **worst** failure here — it stalls every guarded stone
below it. the leaf refusal must never reach a caller with no leaf mark. the rule follows the
mark, never the word "reviewer" in a roleset: the ambient actor carries `reviewer`, and every
everyday `claude` would read as a leaf under a role rule (F23).

a raw `claude` needs no special mark. its tool shell has no leaf mark, so its guard's
`rhx enroll` and its own `rhx review` are admitted — exactly as for a clone `rhx enroll` started.

## .the reported block — what it is, and what it is not

**reproduced 2026-10-07:** `RHACHET_CLONE_DEPTH=1 rhx enroll claude --roles -driver -p '…'` is
refused: `✋ ConstraintError: clone enroll depth budget spent`, `depthRequested: 2`, `depthMax: 1`.

two harms sat behind that one refusal:
- **a worker at depth 1 could run no review.** a storefront worker a top-level clone enrolled
  is depth 1, so every reviewer its guard enrolled was refused, and its stones stalled. this is
  the harm the #577 rule repairs: depth no longer refuses
- **a reviewer could fan out.** a reviewer clone that read as top-level (an absent var, a reset
  through a fresh pty) spawned reviewers of its own. this is the harm the leaf mark repairs: a
  reviewer is refused at every door, whatever its depth and whatever its tty

**the fix:**
- the depth cap is removed at both doors; depth is carried as a fact
- a `-p` enroll and every repl-door call mint a leaf child; the leaf mark rides the child env
- both doors refuse a caller that carries the mark, before any clone dir or process exists

## .the outcome world

### a day in the life

a human opens `claude` or runs `rhx enroll claude` in a terminal pane. that top-level clone drives
a route, and enrolls workers for the stones it hands out. each worker drives its own stone, and
at each guarded stone its guard enrolls reviewers with `-p` and runs `rhx review` — all
allowed, at depth 1 and below. each reviewer reads, judges, and returns. when its rubric tempts
it to fetch a second opinion — `rhx enroll`, or `rhx review` with a repl brain — rhachet refuses
in one loud line that names the reviewer rule, and it answers from its own read. reviews finish
in one pass. the fleet's token spend per stone is bounded by the number of reviewers the guard
asked for, at whatever depth the stone sits.

### before / after

| | before (the depth-1 cap) | after (#577) |
|---|---|---|
| a depth-1 worker's guard enrolls a reviewer | 🔴 refused — its stones stall | ✅ allowed; the reviewer is depth 2, a leaf |
| a depth-1 worker runs `rhx review` (repl) | 🔴 refused | ✅ allowed; the brain is depth 2, a leaf |
| a reviewer (`-p` child) runs `rhx enroll` | refused by depth, unless its depth read low | refused by the leaf mark, whatever its depth: `a reviewer clone may not spawn` |
| a review brain runs `rhx review` or `rhx enroll` | refused by depth, unless its depth read low | refused by the leaf mark |
| a reviewer wraps its enroll in a fresh pty | depth 1 + 1 → refused (F13) | refused — the mark is read at any tty |
| a depth-0 clone runs `rhx review` | ✅ allowed | ✅ allowed; brain carries depth 1 and the leaf mark, no parent serial |
| a raw `claude`'s guard enrolls a reviewer | ✅ allowed, reviewer at depth 1 | ✅ allowed, unchanged — and the reviewer is a leaf |
| a human enrolls at a plain terminal | depth 0 | depth 0 — unchanged |

### the aha

the old cap bounded fan-out by **where** a clone sat in the chain. that one number could not tell
a worker from a reviewer, so it refused the worker that needed to review and let through any
reviewer whose number read low. the fix bounds fan-out by **what** a clone was spawned to do:
- the door that mints a reviewer marks it, once, at spawn
- the mark rides the env to every process the reviewer starts, its shells and jobs included
- both doors read the mark before they spawn, so a reviewer is refused at every door
- depth stays, a fact of the chain, and bounds no spawn

## .the contract we propose

- **one read of depth:** `asCloneSpawnDepth({ env, isTty, door })` returns the depth the new
  clone or brain carries:
  - `door: 'enroll'` with a tty on stdin and no parseable depth var → `0` — a top-level clone
  - else → caller depth + 1, where the caller depth is `0` when the var is absent or unparseable,
    else the var. so a tty that carries a var — a pty a clone opened — records the clone below
    the clone that opened it (F13)
  - so every no-tty spawn is at least depth 1. the repl door never mints 0: a review brain is
    always a spawn (Q8). no `CLAUDECODE` read. no cap: the depth bounds no spawn (#577).
- **one leaf mark:** `RHACHET_CLONE_LEAF` (`CLONE_ENV_KEYS.leaf`). `isCloneSpawnChildLeaf({ door,
  isPrintMode })` decides it for a child — the repl door always, the enroll door when `-p` is
  passed; `isCloneSpawnCallerLeaf({ env })` reads it for a caller — any non-empty value
- **a raw `claude` needs no reset:** in a clean pane the absent var reads as a depth-0 caller; in
  an inherited pane its depth reads one level deep, and refuses no one. a session-start depth
  reset, for an exact record only, is F8 and is not built here. no settings file may carry a
  `RHACHET_CLONE_*` key: the render strips them (F7), so no settings can forge a depth or a mark.
- **two doors, one check:** `rhx enroll` and `asBrainReplWithContextBound` both call
  `assertCloneSpawnAllowed({ isCallerLeaf, door })`, and refuse a leaf caller with one message:
  ```
  ✋ ConstraintError: a reviewer clone may not spawn
    hint: a reviewer is a leaf: it answers, and spawns no clone of its own. … — the full text is
          snapped beside assertCloneSpawnAllowed
    via:  the door, e.g. a clone enroll (`rhx enroll`)
    why:  this process is a reviewer: a print-mode enroll (`rhx enroll -p`) or a repl brain …
  ```
  🔴 a consumer break: the refusal reads `a reviewer clone may not spawn` with metadata
  `{ hint, via, why }`, where main reads `clone enroll depth budget spent` with
  `{ depthRequested, depthMax }`
- **`--async`:** the depth is decided in the foreground, where the tty is, and handed to the
  detached host, which has no tty and must not re-read.
- **propagation at the repl door:** for the duration of a repl `ask`/`act`, `process.env` carries
  the child depth, the leaf mark, and no parent serial/socket. it is restored after the last
  concurrent call returns.
  - 🔴 **the caller is read once, before the scope opens, and held by the scope.** a call that
    starts while a peer's scope is open is never checked against the env the peer set. otherwise
    a work clone's second parallel rubric would read the leaf mark its first rubric set, and be
    refused (the worst failure).
  - enroll paths already propagate (`genBrainCliPtyClone`, `genBrainCliPlainClone`,
    `genCloneEnrollDetached`).
  - the depth half is what the wish requires. the serial/socket drop is a safe ride-along (F5),
    not a wish requirement, and the council may strike it.
- **atoms pass:** one api request, no process, no tools.

## .evaluation

- **goals (per #577):**
  - a work clone spawns at depth 0, 1 and beyond ✅
  - a reviewer (leaf) is refused loud, names the reviewer rule ✅
  - the leaf mark survives enroll (`-p`, the detached host, a pty wrap) and repl ✅
  - depth survives enroll (extant) and repl, as a fact ✅
  - a clone a human starts at a clean tty is depth 0; a tty that inherited a var records var + 1 ✅
  - a raw `claude`'s reviewers, a cron job's clones, and a human's review brains are depth 1,
    and the reviewers and review brains are leaves ✅
  - tests prove cases 1–7 ✅
- **pros:** one seam per door; no supplier change; the extant enroll check and tests stay; no
  launcher or flag for a human to learn.
- **cons:**
  - a process-wide env mutation for the length of a repl call (F2)
  - a no-tty caller never starts a depth-0 clone — a record only, since depth refuses no one
- **edge cases + pit of success:**
  - concurrent repl calls → refcounted scope, restored only after the last
  - unparseable depth var → reads as a depth-0 caller, so its child is depth 1
  - a leaf mark on an empty string → reads as no mark; a work child carries no key at all, so a
    pty never writes `'undefined'` that would read as a leaf
  - `--async` → depth decided in the foreground, handed to the host

## .open questions & assumptions

- **assumption:** every repl a role package calls goes through `genContextBrain`. a role that
  imports a supplier directly bypasses the seam (named as impossible in the inventory).
- **assumption:** a brain's tool shell has no tty on stdin, so a brain-run `rhx enroll` never
  mints depth 0. `test -t 0` exits 1 from the `Bash` tool of an enrolled clone that itself runs
  in a pty (F6).
- **Q9 [answered — wisher, S1]** — a raw `claude` is a top-level clone, the same as a top-level
  `rhx enroll`. its spawns have no tty, so they are depth 1.
- **Q8 [answered — wisher, S1]** — a human's own `rhx review` mints its brain at depth 1. a review
  brain is a spawn, never top-level.
- **Q7 [answered — wisher, S1]** — (a) the wisher's launch terminal carries no depth var, so the
  inherited pane is an edge, not the wisher's path. (b) a `!rhx enroll` typed into a clone's
  session has no tty, so it is that clone's spawn: depth 1. this follows the rule; no flag.
- **Q1 [research — gated on Q11]** — a `claude` opened in an inherited pane is no longer refused
  *if* its session starts at depth 0 (contract). that rests on Q11, and on the repo brain dir's
  settings rendered in the repo where the `claude` opens. a repo without that render gets no
  reset, so its raw `claude` in an inherited pane is still refused — the refusal hint must say so,
  and execution must test it.
- **Q11 [research]** — does a `claude` settings `env` key override a var the process already
  inherited? measure in execution before the contract relies on it. if not, the SessionStart
  `CLAUDE_ENV_FILE` hook is the fallback.
- **Q10 [research]** — does any other brain CLI's shell tool hand its commands a tty? if so, its
  depth-1 clone's `rhx enroll` would mint depth 0. measure each installed supplier in execution,
  as `test -t 0` was measured for claude. the same edge: a cron job a human runs at a terminal
  passes its tty stdin to an `rhx enroll` it shells out to — accepted, as a human at the keyboard.
- **Q2 [answered]** — should atoms count? no: an atom is one api request with no process and no
  tools, so it cannot spawn (logic). F3 holds it for the council.
- **Q3 [answered]** — does the review skill go through `genContextBrain`? yes:
  `review.js:349`, `genReviewBrainSupply.js:63`, and `choice` is wrapped at
  `genContextBrain.ts:246-252` (code).
- **Q4 [answered]** — does `act` need the same check as `ask`? yes: `act` grants `Bash`
  (`genBrainRepl.js:67`), so it is the more able spawner (code).
- **Q5 [research]** — do the other installed suppliers (e.g. `rhachet-brains-openai`) read
  `process.env` within the call, as anthropic does? to check in execution, before the seam
  relies on it; if one snapshots env early, the scope misses it (F2).
- **Q6 [research]** — how does bhrain's `rhx review` render a `ConstraintError` thrown from
  `choice.ask` — loud with exit 2, or wrapped as a malfunction? the refusal must reach the
  reviewer legibly; check in execution with a depth-1 env.

## .what is awkward

- **a mutation of `process.env` in a library seam** — it works because suppliers read the env,
  but it is a side effect a reader would not expect from a context binder. F2 names the cleaner
  supplier-contract alternative and why it is deferred.
- **a tty as a depth signal** — it marks a human at a keyboard, not a human's intent. a human who
  pipes into `rhx enroll` loses the tty and is counted one level down. the wisher set this trade
  (S1): no tty is depth 1.
- **the spawn vocab** — the depth read and the leaf check serve both doors, so they are named for
  a spawn, never an enroll: `asCloneSpawnDepth` and `assertCloneSpawnAllowed`.
- **the leaf mark is one env key a process can unset** — a reviewer that runs `env -u
  RHACHET_CLONE_LEAF rhx enroll` escapes the rule. the mark bounds rhachet's doors against an honest brain, not a hostile one.
- **two impossibles stay open** — direct supplier imports and claude `Task` subagents spawn
  outside rhachet's seams. the fix bounds rhachet clones, not every brain a brain can start.
  the bound: rhachet's two spawn seams are `invokeEnroll` and `asBrainReplWithContextBound`
  (`rhx grepsafe --pattern 'asBrainReplWithContextBound\(' --path src`: every repl that
  `genContextBrain` hands out is wrapped there, at `genContextBrain.ts:251,273,282`); a role
  package that imports a supplier's repl directly, or a `Task` subagent claude runs in-process,
  calls neither.
