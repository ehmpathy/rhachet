# case=3 — a depth-0 driver's review brain carries depth 1 and the leaf mark

**cells:** depth0 × repl (solo and concurrent) → spawned; child at depth 1, a leaf, no parent serial
**feel × care:** 😊 for the driver, 🗡️ for the child · 🔴 critipath — the propagation half

## .the narrative

a depth-0 driver runs `rhx review`. the repl seam allows it (the driver carries no leaf mark).
for the duration of the supplier call, the env the supplier hands its brain carries
`RHACHET_CLONE_DEPTH=1` and `RHACHET_CLONE_LEAF=1`, and no `RHACHET_CLONE_SERIAL` /
`RHACHET_CLONE_SOCKET` — those name the driver, and the review brain is not the driver.

inside the review brain, an attempt at `rhx enroll` or a nested `rhx review` reads the leaf mark
and is refused (case=1, case=2). the mark went down; it was never dropped.

after the call returns, the driver's own `process.env` reads depth 0, no leaf mark, and its own
serial again.

**why this matters even though review uses `ask`:** the anthropic supplier denies `Bash` to an
`ask` brain (`genBrainRepl.js:56,179`), so today's review brain cannot shell out at all. but an
`act` brain may (`TOOLS_ALLOWED_FOR_ACT`), and another supplier may grant tools to `ask`. rhachet
cannot rely on a supplier's tool list to hold the leaf rule; the mark must ride down regardless.

## .the sketch

```
given('[case3] a driver clone at depth 0, serial S')
  when('[t0] the driver runs a brain via repl that holds a shell (an `act` brain), and that brain runs `rhx enroll`')
    then('the brain sees "✋ ConstraintError: a reviewer clone may not spawn", exit 2 — fast and loud')
    then('the message names the door and why the process is a reviewer — it teaches the fix')
    then('the brain finishes its task from its own read; the driver gets its answer')
  when('[t1] the driver reads its own env after the review')
    then('the review brain was handed no serial and no socket, so no clone but the driver answers to serial S')
    then('the driver still reads as depth 0, no leaf mark, serial S — its own spawns still pass')
  when('[t2] the driver runs several such brains in parallel, and each runs `rhx enroll`')
    then('every one of the N parallel calls is allowed — none reads the leaf mark from a peer scope still open')
    then('every brain is refused the same way — none loses the mark because a peer finished first')
  when('[t3] a caller with no tty and no depth var (a cron job, or a raw claude tool shell) runs N repl calls in parallel')
    then('every child carries depth 1 and the leaf mark — every call reads the same held caller')
    then('every call is allowed — a work caller is never refused')
```

proof: `withCloneSpawnDepthScope.test.ts` · `asBrainReplWithContextBound.integration.test.ts` ·
`blackbox/sdk/replDoorRealSupplier.realnode.integration.test.ts` · `blackbox/sdk/replDoorRealSupplier.realnode.acceptance.test.ts` (live).

impl note: the probe for [t0]–[t2] is a supplier stub that records the env it was handed —
`RHACHET_CLONE_DEPTH=1`, `RHACHET_CLONE_LEAF=1`, no `RHACHET_CLONE_SERIAL` /
`RHACHET_CLONE_SOCKET`; the scope is refcounted and restored after the last concurrent call
returns (F2, F5). the leaf check runs only when the scope opens; a call that joins an open scope
is never checked against the env the scope wrote.
