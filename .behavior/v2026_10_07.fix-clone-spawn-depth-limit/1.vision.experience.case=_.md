# 1.vision.experience.case=_ — the inventory

every cell of D1 (caller) × D2 (path) × D3 (concurrency), verdicted. outcome column = after the fix.

the rule, per the ehmpathy/rhachet#577 vision (the S68 verdict): **a leaf may not spawn; every
other caller may, at any depth.** a `-p` enroll's child and every repl-door child is a leaf. depth
is a fact, never a limit: an enroll at a tty whose env holds no parseable depth var is depth 0;
every other spawn is caller depth + 1, where a no-tty caller with no var or an unparseable var
counts as depth 0. a `tty-inherited` caller reads its var + 1 at both doors, as a no-tty caller
does (F13).

the `today` column names main, the build this pr changes, which refuses every enroll from a
caller at depth ≥ 1 and checks no repl call.

feel: 😊 happy · 🗡️ sharp. care: 🔴 critipath · ⚪ alterpath.

verdicts: **demoed** (a case file) · **itemized** (named here) · **forbidden** (a nurture
invariant the execution must hold — here, *a leaf caller may not spawn*, why = the #577 rule: a
reviewer answers, and spawns no clone of its own) · **impossible** (no rhachet seam reaches it).
