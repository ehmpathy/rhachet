# case=6 — a fleet peer review, end to end

**cells walked:** tty-inherited × enroll.watch → depth0 × enroll.await → depth1 × repl → refused
**feel × care:** 😊 for the driver, 🗡️ for the reviewer · 🔴 critipath — the densest walk, and
the one that stalls the fleet today

## .the narrative

a human, in a terminal pane that inherited a clone's `RHACHET_CLONE_DEPTH`, runs
`rhx enroll claude --roles mechanic,driver` and asks it to drive a route. the driver arrives at
`5.1.execution`. the guard runs
`rhx enroll claude --roles reviewer,behaver,architect,mechanic -p '<review the diff>'`.

**today:** the driver was born at depth 1 from the inherited var, so the guard's enroll mints
depth 2 and is refused (case=4). the stone stalls. and on a box where the driver is truly depth 0,
the reviewer is depth 1 but may still run `rhx review`, which walks the repl door unchecked and
starts one brain per rubric. either way the guard's `-p` stalls.

**after:**

1. the human's enroll sees a tty → the driver is depth 0 (case=4)
2. the guard's enroll has no tty → reads depth 0 → the reviewer is minted at depth 1 (case=1)
3. the reviewer runs `rhx review`. the repl seam reads depth 1 → child depth 2 > 1 → refused,
   before the supplier is called (case=2):
   ```
   ✋ ConstraintError: clone spawn depth limit reached
     depth: 1 (the limit to spawn is depth 0)
     hint:  a clone at depth 1 may not spawn another — ask the clone that spawned you, or a human
   ```
4. the reviewer reads one line, reviews the diff itself, prints its verdict
5. the guard's `-p` returns inside one brain's time. the driver reads the verdict and moves on

three boundaries in one walk — human/clone (the tty read), enroll (the depth marker), repl (the
new check) — the top-level spawn is allowed, and the refusal lands only at the deepest one.

## 🔴 .amended by F13 — for the wisher

as built (F13, candidate (i)), step 1 holds only at a terminal with no depth var: a tty that
inherited one reads that var + 1 (case=4, amended). so from an inherited pane, the driver is minted
at depth 1 and the guard's enroll at step 2 is refused. the walk above holds from a fresh terminal
(`tty-clean`), which the refusal hint names. the reported stall returns for an inherited pane, as a
loud refusal with a cure, in trade for no silent reset to depth 0 through a fresh pty.

## .the sketch

```
given('[case6] a human at a tty whose env inherited a clone depth enrolls a driver')
  when('[t0] the driver arrives at a guarded stone, and the guard enrolls a reviewer with `-p`')
    then('the reviewer boots at depth 1 — the guard was not refused')
  when('[t1] the reviewer runs `rhx review`, as its rubric asks')
    then('it sees "✋ ConstraintError: clone spawn depth limit reached", exit 2')
    then('no review brain starts under it')
  when('[t2] the reviewer writes its verdict from its own read')
    then('the guard returns inside one brain time, and the driver reads the verdict')
```

impl note: [t0] rides the tty read (case=4); [t1] rides the repl seam check on
`brain.choice.ask` (case=2).
