# 1.vision.experience.case=7.promote-or-drop — happy alterpath

## .narrative

after a week, vlad's short-sentences shadow earned its keep and his buzzwords `not` did not. he
opens a pr upstream with the shadow's file, and deletes the `not:` line here.

when ehmpathy releases mechanic@1.41.0 with his change, `rhx upgrade` names the move and the boot
notice names the drift (case 5). he diffs his shadow against the new upstream, sees no difference, and deletes his file.
with the last mod gone he deletes the mod dir; `rhx init` returns the mechanic to per-dir links.

**why alterpath**: the fallback is to leave a redundant mod in place — it renders the same content
either way. the drop is hygiene, not a rescue.

## .bdd `[tn]` timeline

```
given a shadow of short-sentences and a `not:` of buzzwords

  when [t0] vlad deletes the `not:` line
    then the next boot renders the buzzwords brief again, as published

     and he deletes the shadow file and sets of: ehmpathy/mechanic@1.41.0

  when [t2] that was the last mod, and he deletes the mod dir and runs `rhx init`
    then the mechanic's view returns to per-dir links, and `rhx roles cost` drops the `[~]` marker
```
