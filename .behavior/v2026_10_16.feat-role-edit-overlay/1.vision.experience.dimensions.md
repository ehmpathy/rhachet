# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

> the axes the role-mod experience varies over. each is a small closed set; each is orthogonal to
> the others. the product is walked, cell by cell, in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`.

## .the dimensions

| # | dimension | values | why it is its own axis |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **actor** | `author` (a human or a supervisor clone that writes the mod) · `clone` (boots under the modded role) · `reviewer` (reads the diff or the boot to see what changed) | who meets the mod decides what they must see. an author writes; a clone only reads; a reviewer audits |
| D2 | **goal** | `shadow` an original · `add` a new file · `not` an original · `inspect` what is modded · `upgrade` under a mod · `retire` a mod (promote upstream, or drop) | six distinct reasons to touch the mod dir. the first three are the wisher's three ops (S1) |
| D3 | **target state** — what the mod names, measured against the published role | `held` (the role ships that path) · `unheld` (the role ships no such path) · `gone` (the role shipped it once, ships it no more) | the state the match rule acts on. `unheld` is where a typo hides; `gone` only arises after an upgrade |
| D4 | **role identity** — what the mod's `of:` names | `one` (a linked `repo/role`) · `many` (a bare slug two linked repos share) · `none` (no linked role, or `of:` absent) | independent of what the mod names inside the role |
| D5 | **artifact kind** — what the mod file is | `brief` · `skill` · the mod's `boot.yml` · `init` (hooks included) · `keyrack.yml` · `readme` | briefs and skills are in scope (S1); each other kind needs its own verdict |
| D6 | **boot placement** — where the modded content lands in the render | `say` · `ref` · `unplaced` (neither) | the wish's own caution: an addition a boot never loads mods naught |
| D7 | **clone time** — when the clone's session began, relative to the mod | `new` (enrolled after) · `live` (session began before) | a clone reads boot.md at session start; independent of what the mod holds |
| D8 | **same slug, other repo** — does another linked repo, or `.this`, ship the modded role's slug | `alone` · `shared` | roles boot apart (S4), so a shared slug means two roles beside each other, and `of:` must pick one |
| D9 | **version** — the `@version` in the mod `boot.yml`'s `of:` against the installed role | `equal` · `differs` · `absent` | drives the onStop pester (S2); independent of the files the mod holds |

## .why these are orthogonal

- **D3 × D6** — a `held` shadow keeps the placement of the original unless the mod's `boot.yml`
  names it (read first, first-say-wins); an `unheld` addition takes the slot its declaration names, or
  is `unplaced` and refused. either state can meet any placement
- **D4 × D3** — role identity is fixed before any path match. a `many` or `none` role never reaches a
  D3 verdict; the grid shows that as a whole row of `forbidden`, not a correlation
- **D2 × D5** — every op applies to both in-scope kinds: a brief or a skill may be shadowed, added or
  `not`'d. the out-of-scope kinds are one refusal each, whatever the op
- **D9 × D3** — the version says the role moved; D3 says what that did to each file. a `differs`
  version with every path still `held` is the common case: the pester asks for a review, and naught
  is refused

## .what the grid surfaced

- **no hashes ⇒ no `moved` state.** an earlier walk had a `moved` value (the role ships the path, but
  its content changed since the mod was cut), observable only through a per-file content hash. the
  wisher withdrew hashes for simplicity (S2), so `moved` collapses into `held` per file — and D9's
  `differs` is what stands in for it, coarse but on the role grain
- **a skill runs from where its peers are.** a skill sources its peer files from its own dir
  (`git.commit.sponsor.sh:29-37`). the walk of `shadow × skill` forced the merged view: a modded
  role's `briefs/` and `skills/` become per-file links, so a shadow runs beside the published peers.
  not an axis — the mechanism that makes `shadow × skill` work at all

## .the product

`3 × 6 × 3 × 3 × 6 × 3 × 2 × 2 × 3` raw tuples. most collapse: D5 out of scope is one verdict per
kind, D4 ≠ `one` short-circuits before D3, D6 is set by the original for a shadow, and D7, D8, D9 each
add one row of their own. the reduced walk — **actor × goal × target state**, with D4–D9 walked as
their own short rows — is in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`.
