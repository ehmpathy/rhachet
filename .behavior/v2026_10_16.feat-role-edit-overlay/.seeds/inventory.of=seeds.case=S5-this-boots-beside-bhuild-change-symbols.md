asked where the `~` marker comes from, the wisher answered in three steps:

> declastruct

> and bhuild

> yeah conform to the bhuild symbols in general, enrul that

## .settled

- a `.this` role boots beside a same-slug package role. "roles boot independently" holds with no
  exception
- a change is marked with the bhuild bracket legend — `[+]` create, `[~]` update, `[○]` retain,
  `[-]` delete, `[←]` reuse, `[→]` eject — everywhere, never a coined glyph. a modded role and a
  shadow are `[~]`, an add `[+]`, a `not` `[-]`
