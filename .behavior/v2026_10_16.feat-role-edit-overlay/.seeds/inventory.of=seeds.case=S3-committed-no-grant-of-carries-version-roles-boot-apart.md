- `of: repo/role[@version]` is the one added key. the `@version` is optional and drives the pester
- the premise of the last answer holds for the cli (`roles boot --role`, `run --skill` refuse a
  same-slug ambiguity) and not for a clone's `boot.md`, which boots one role per slug and drops the
