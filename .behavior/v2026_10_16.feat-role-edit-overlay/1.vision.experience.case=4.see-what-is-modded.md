# 1.vision.experience.case=4.see-what-is-modded — happy critipath

## .narrative

three readers, one question: *is this the published role, or a modded one — and what changed?*

- **the clone** reads its boot.md. above the mechanic's briefs sits one notice: the role is modded,
  with counts, and the `not`'d paths listed (a removal leaves no resource to tag). each shadow and
  addition carries `mod="shadow"` or `mod="add"`. a clone that cites a rule can tell a local mod
  from the role's law.
- **the reviewer** on the pr sees the mod dir as plain files plus one `boot.yml` — the pr diff is
  the mod. to read a shadow against upstream, a plain `diff` of the two paths does it.
- **the human** runs `rhx roles cost` and sees `[~] modded` beside the mechanic, with counts. no file
  under `.agent/repo=.mods/` can change a boot and stay invisible there.

**why critipath**: the wish forbids a silent divergence. if visibility broke, a modded role would
read as the published one, and a rule a clone obeys would have no traceable source.

## .bdd `[tn]` timeline

```
given the mechanic carries 1 shadow (short-sentences), 1 addition (commit-scope-from-diff),
  and 1 not (buzzwords)

  when [t0] a clone boots
    then boot.md opens the mechanic section with a notice:
         "role ehmpathy/mechanic@1.39.5 is modded in this repo — 1 shadow, 1 add, 1 not
          (.agent/repo=.mods/role=mechanic)", which lists the not'd path
     and the shadow's tag carries mod="shadow", the addition's mod="add"
     and no buzzwords brief renders

  when [t1] the human runs `rhx roles cost`
    then the mechanic row reads `[~] modded · 1 shadow · 1 add · 1 not`
     and an unmodded role shows no marker

  when [t2] the mod dir is removed and `rhx init` runs
    then the mechanic's view returns to per-dir links, the marker drops,
         and the next boot renders the role as published
```
