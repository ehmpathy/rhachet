## 🔴 .every entry lists the demos that RENDER it — or declares that none does

> **a demo that renders a shape the contract did not choose is a contract change nobody voted for.**

the mechanism, and it is specific to this route rather than general prudence: **the `2.1.criteria`
stone seeds its bdd from the `case=N` demos.** so a shape a demo renders as plain fact becomes an
assertion, and an assertion is a settled call — reached with no verdict from the wisher on the
fulcrum that reserved it. that is `rule.always.raise-a-blocker-a-taken-cannot-close` exactly: *"to
argue an itemized fulcrum down settles by side effect a question you reserved, and the wisher never
learns it was asked twice."*

⇒ so each entry carries a `.the demos that RENDER this call` section, and each listed demo names the
fulcrum **inline, as unruled**, on the line that renders it.

| the entry states | means |
|---|---|
| a table of demos, each `✅ marked` | the call is reserved everywhere it is spoken |
| **NONE**, explicitly | walked and absent — a verdict changes no demo (F04) |
| the section is **absent** | 🔴 unwalked. an absent row and an unchecked one render identically |

🟡 **F03 is the one deliberate exception, and it states why:** all seven demos render its subject, so
a per-line marker would be noise (`rule.forbid.emphasis-noise`) and the claim is the vision's
premise rather than a local shape. it is declared once, in that entry.
