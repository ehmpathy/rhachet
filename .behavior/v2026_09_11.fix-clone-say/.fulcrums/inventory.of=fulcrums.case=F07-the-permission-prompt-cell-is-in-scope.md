## .the fork, stated fairly

the dimensional walk surfaced a cell the issue does not name: a brain-cli parked on a permission
prompt receives our paste **at the prompt**, and `say`'s own `\r` then **answers it**. an unattended
daemon can therefore approve a tool call no human approved — and today's `say` reports `delivered`,
exit 0, because a dismissed prompt ends the turn and the transcript moves on.

| option | cost |
|---|---|
| **A** — in scope. the pre-check refuses on a modal (case=6), and the verify's two reads catch the post-check race as `absent` | enlarges a wish the wisher scoped to a delivery defect, and adds a critipath with a demo owed |
| **B** — out of scope. record it, seed a separate wish, ship the delivery fix alone | a known safety hole ships with the very release that built the instrument to close it |
| **C** — in scope, but pre-check only (no `absent` verdict for the race) | cheaper, and it leaves the residual silent — the race would read as `queued`, a green lie on an eaten message |

## .taken, and why at the time

**A**, on three grounds:

1. **same cause.** the prompt defect and the delivery defect are both *"`say` writes blind"*. the
   yield's root drill reaches one root for three defects
2. **same fix.** the pre-check that case=2 needs is the same read that case=6 needs — one enum field
   (`focus: 'input' | 'modal'`) on a reply that must exist anyway. the marginal cost is one branch
3. 🔴 **the race makes A and C not equivalent.** a modal can appear *between* the pre-check and the
   write, and that residual is only catchable by the verify's two-read conjunct. so the `absent`
   verdict is not extra scope — it is what stops the race from a false green
