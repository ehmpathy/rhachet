# case=2 — a say refuses to paste over a human's half-typed message

`unattended × input-dirty × (refused before any write) × probe-capable` · **feel** sharp ·
**care** critipath (freq: any daemon nudge that races a human's keystrokes · cost: a human's
in-flight words destroyed, unrecoverably)

⇒ the cell the walk surfaced. the issue files it **secondary**; the box ranks it **second-highest by
cost**, because it is one of only two cells that lose a human's work rather than a nudge (case=6 is
the other, and its cost is unbounded).

🟡 **by boundary-density it is rank 6= — one boundary, the pre-check.** the two numbers disagree on
purpose: `define.experience._.metric=boundary-density` says density sets *priority and scrutiny* and
care sets *severity*, so a cost argument is made as a cost argument. this case keeps a full spotlight
on **cost** grounds, and case=7 is what carries the **density**.

## .the narrative — the human's pov

i am mid-sentence in my clone's input box. i have typed *"ok so the fulcrum on F03 is really about
whether the invariant"* — a long thought, half of it still in my head.

**today** a reminder daemon fires at that exact moment. it writes blind. my half-sentence is still
in the box, so its paste lands on the end of mine, and then its `\r` submits **both, merged**:

```
> ok so the fulcrum on F03 is really about whether the invariantyou can do it! drive on, fulcrum, and converge
```

my clone now answers a question i never asked. my actual thought is gone — not queued, not
recoverable, **gone**, and i cannot even reconstruct it because the screen no longer holds what i
had typed. the daemon reports `delivered` and exits 0, so no log anywhere says my work was eaten.

**post-fix** the daemon probes first, sees the box is dirty with text it did not author, and
refuses to write. my half-sentence sits untouched. i finish my thought, submit it, and the daemon's
next tick lands cleanly.

## .the narrative — the daemon's pov

i get exit 2 and, on stderr, `✋ ConstraintError: the clone input box holds uncommitted text (a
human mid-type) — the say was withheld`. i did not deliver, and i know it, so i tick again in a
minute. that is the correct outcome: a nudge
delayed a minute costs the driver no ground; a nudge that eats a human's sentence costs their trust
in the whole clone surface.

## .how it fails safe — fast · loud · teaches the fix

| property | how |
|---|---|
| **fast** | refused on the probe, **before** the write. no paste, no `\r`, no partial state to unwind |
| **loud** | exit 2 and a named verdict `withheld`. never a silent skip (`rule.forbid.failhide`) and never a false `delivered` |
| **teaches the fix** | the error names both moves: retry when clear, or `--force` with its cost stated in the same breath |

## .why the default is `refuse`, not `force`

the easy path must be the correct path (`rule.require.safe-by-default`). the two failure costs are
not symmetric:

- **refuse, wrongly** → one nudge delayed by a tick. recoverable, and the daemon retries by design
- **force, wrongly** → a human's words destroyed with no record. unrecoverable

⇒ so `force` is the deliberate extra step, and its help text states what it destroys rather than
merely what it overrides.

🔴 **a forced dispatch destroys TWO things, not one.** the extant write appends, so it does not
replace the human's fragment — it lands after it, and the `\r` submits `<their fragment><our message>`
as **one** turn:

| the cost | |
|---|---|
| the human's words are destroyed | the fragment is swept into a turn they did not submit |
| 🔴 **our own message is ALTERED** — the brain answers a prompt neither party wrote | and `say` reports `released`, exit 0, because the needle count rises on the fused turn |

⇒ **a flag chosen on an incomplete cost is `rule.forbid.obfuscation`**, and this one is elected by a
daemon that cannot see the screen it forced a write onto. the requirement, and the `Q22` measurement it
turns on: the yield's `.--force APPENDS`.

## 🟡 .the honest bound on this case

`say` can see that the line is dirty and that **our** needle is not in it. it cannot see *whose*
text it is — a human's keystrokes and a prior stuck paste render identically. that ambiguity is
acceptable in this direction: both warrant a refusal, and the refusal names the observation
(`input-region-dirty`) rather than a guess about its author.
