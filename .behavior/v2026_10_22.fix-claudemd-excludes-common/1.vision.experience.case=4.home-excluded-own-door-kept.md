# case=4 — the home CLAUDE.md is excluded; the actor's own door is kept (sharp)

## narrative — the human's walk

vlad keeps personal notes in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` — his tone, his shortcuts. he launches claude
against an actor's settings with no config dir (the way the journey test probes an actor). the
brain answers from the actor's boot and knows none of his notes. on camper's mac, at
`/Users/camper`, the same committed line does the same. the actor's own boot, which lives in
`<actor>/brain/.claude/CLAUDE.md`, still loads — a pattern broad enough to hit every home is not
broad enough to hit the actor.

