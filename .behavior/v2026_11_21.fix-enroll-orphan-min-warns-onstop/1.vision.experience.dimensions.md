# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

## .dimensions

| # | dimension | positions |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | defect kind | `orphan` (an `.md.min` with no `.md`) · `boundary` (a brief holds the cache-boundary literal) · `spec` (the spec parse refuses `boot.yml`: invalid yaml, or yaml a schema check refuses — two modes mixed, a key no render reads such as `inherits`, a budget that is no whole number. one kind, one set-aside: the role boots refs-only) · `none` · `identity` (role not linked, focus absent, mod stale or native-targeted) · `malfunction` (render not delivered, a file vanished mid-read) |
| D2 | owner of the defect role | `owned` (`.agent/repo=.this`) · `linked` (a package role) |
| D3 | moment | `birth` (`rhx enroll`) · `rerender` (`rhx init`, `rhx upgrade`, declared actors — each via `setBrainDirBoot`) · `stop` (`roles cost --all --when hook.onStop`) · `session-boot` (`roles boot` from a hook) · `hand` (`roles cost --all`) · `publish` (`repo introspect`) |
| D4 | sweep armed | `armed` (a `.this` budget or a mod, and the clone keeps the carrier role) · `unarmed` (the repo arms none, or the clone's roles drop the carrier) |

D1's first three positions are the **content** class; `identity` and `malfunction` are the classes the seam keeps as halts (yield `.the seam`). they are on the axis so their halt cells are walked, not hidden.

## .orthogonality, per axis

- **D1 ⟂ D2**: any defect kind can sit in an owned or a linked role. one declared correlation: D2 has no referent where D1 = `none` or `malfunction` (no defect role). folded by R4, R5
- **D1 ⟂ D3**: every kind can meet every moment; the moment decides the response, the kind decides the set-aside form
- **D2 ⟂ D3**: an owned or linked role is rendered at every moment
- **D4 ⟂ D1, D2**: arm state is a fact of the repo and the clone: the repo arms the sweep, and a clone whose roles drop the carrier role drops the hook from its settings, so it is unarmed in an armed repo (`isCloneStopSweepArmed` [t2]). either way it is independent of which role holds which defect. one declared correlation: D4 bears only on D3 = `stop` (no other moment reads the hook). folded by R1
- **concurrency / time is not an axis**, and here is why it cannot fork the experience: each render reads every said file once (`getAllBootSayResources`), and a file that vanishes mid-read is the `malfunction` position, already walked. at the stop, the fingerprint is taken before the sweep and only a clean verdict is memoized; an edit that lands mid-sweep changes the next stop's fingerprint, so the next stop reruns. a repair that races a stop is at worst one stale hold, cured on the next stop. no path yields a clean memo over a live defect

## .the walked product

6 × 2 × 6 × 2 = 144 cells. the fold rules:

- **R1.** D4 bears only on `stop`; at every other moment, armed and unarmed cells are one
- **R2.** at `birth` and `rerender`, every content kind behaves alike: set aside, render the rest, 🟡 receipt. only the set-aside form differs by kind (skip · defang · refs-only). at `session-boot` no clone is born, so it departs per kind (row 10)
- **R3.** at `stop`: `owned` + `armed` holds (exit 2); `owned` + `unarmed` has no hook; `linked` never holds, and warns by a JSON reply where armed
- **R4.** D1 = `none` is silent at every moment; D2 has no referent
- **R5.** D1 = `identity` or `malfunction` halts at every render moment, unchanged by this work (forbidden to warn)
- **R6.** at `publish`, a supplier's own gate: the orphan refusal stays (`assertRegistryHasNoOrphanBriefs`), since it stops the defect at its source before any consumer meets it

the 144 cells fold into rows 1–15 and 18–20 of `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`; `linked` × `publish` is impossible (a repo publishes only its own roles), and `identity` at a moment that asks for no actor is impossible (row 19). row 16 composes several cells into one walk (the densest); row 17 is the clamp, a test over the cells rather than a cell.
